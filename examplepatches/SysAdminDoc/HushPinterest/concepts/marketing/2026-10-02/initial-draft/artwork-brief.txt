HushPinterest README artwork

Drawn in code by make_art.py (Pillow and NumPy), so the art can be rebuilt or
tweaked without an image model. Run it from any directory; it writes both
masters into selected/, which are then copied to assets/icon.png and
assets/readme-hero.png.

Logo
Circular crimson emblem in the Hush family layout: a red ring around a deep
red disc, a bold white H, and a pink push pin stuck through the H's crossbar.
Keep it distinct from Pinterest's own mark (no P, no script lettering). The
canvas outside the circle is transparent with real alpha. 1254 pixels square.

Hero
Wide dark banner, 1672 by 941, RGB. The emblem on the left, the HushPinterest
wordmark, the line "Keep the pins. Lose the ads." and "Morphe patches for
Pinterest on Android" beneath it. A faded masonry grid of blank pin cards
runs down the right side. No real pins, accounts or version number.
