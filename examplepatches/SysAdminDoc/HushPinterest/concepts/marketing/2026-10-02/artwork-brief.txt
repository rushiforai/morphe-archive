HushPinterest README artwork

Selected family identity
The circular HushTelegram emblem is the family reference. HushPinterest keeps
its white beveled H and polished circular rim, changes the colors to crimson
and ruby, and uses a diagonal red push pin. The rim, letter and pin remain
legible on a light or dark surface. The logo is 1254 by 1254, RGBA, with real
transparent pixels outside the emblem.

Hero
The selected 1672 by 941 RGB banner follows HushTelegram's left-text and
right-art composition. It uses a dark burgundy backdrop, the selected emblem,
and tall glass image cards in staggered columns with a crimson light ribbon.
The copy reads HUSHPINTEREST, MORPHE PATCHES FOR PINTEREST, and
"Keep the pins. Lose the ads."
No real pins, accounts or version number.

Files
selected/logo-master.png and selected/hero-master.png are the original selected
masters. The assets/ copies are identical. artwork-prompts.txt contains the
complete briefs. initial-draft/ preserves the earlier code-drawn logo and hero,
their original brief, the source script and the README before this update.
Running that archived script only changes files inside initial-draft/.
