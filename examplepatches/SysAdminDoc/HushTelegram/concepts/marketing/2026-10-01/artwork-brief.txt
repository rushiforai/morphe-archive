HushTelegram README artwork

Explored five logo directions: minimal glyph, app tile, wordmark, circular
emblem, and abstract folded H. The circular emblem is selected. Its bright
ring and folded plane give Telegram a distinct mark within the Hush family.
The other four directions and the earlier hero are in alternates/.

Logo
Circular cobalt emblem with a luminous cyan ring and a bold white H. A folded
cyan paper plane crosses the H. Keep it distinct from Telegram's official mark.
The canvas outside the circle is transparent with real alpha. The mark must read
at 36 pixels on light and dark backgrounds. No words or version number.

Hero
Wide, dark navy banner in the Hush family layout. Place the selected emblem
and wordmark on the left, with a blue-to-cyan rule and the line MORPHE PATCHES
FOR TELEGRAM. The headline reads KEEP THE CHAT. on the first line and CUT THE
NOISE. on the second. On the right, abstract channel cards and a paper plane
follow a cyan light trail. Use no real messages, accounts, or version number.
