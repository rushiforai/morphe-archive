# Changelog

## Unreleased

* **Tooling:** The source audit reads Jman's Patch Bundle Registry through its new API and asks again with fewer rows when a page fails. It also reads every page of a GitHub code search now. Before, it stopped at the first page that came back short and missed files.
* **Tooling:** A release checks the source census when it's cut and again before anything goes up on GitHub. A census refreshed after the cut no longer keeps the publish step from running.

## 0.0.10 (2026-11-06)

* The patches move from 46.8.1.100 to Tumblr 47.0.0.108, the newest release. Every patch applies to it, so that's the version to patch.
* The bundle is built on Morphe patcher 2.3.0 now, the one that comes with Morphe Manager 2.5.0 and Morphe Desktop 2.1.0. Update Manager to 2.5.0 or newer before you add this release, since older Manager builds can't load it.
* Dark background color now reaches blog and tag pages. Those pages paint their owner's color, so a cream blog or a teal tag used to show through the gaps between black posts. A blog's title or link color that wouldn't show on the new background turns white or near black. The screens where you edit your own blog's colors still show the real ones.
* New **Line between posts** switch on the Interface page, off by default. It draws a thin line in the gap between posts, so it's easier to see where one ends on a black background.
* New **Hide Featured posts** switch on the Feed page, off by default. It takes out the posts Tumblr's staff push into your dashboard under a Featured header. A post goes only when Tumblr marks it both ways, with the Featured icon and the menu choice Tumblr gives its own promotions, so a regular post with a similar header stays. Quick setups' Quiet choice turns it on too.
* New **Hide your own reblogs** switch on the Feed page, off by default. It takes the reblogs you made out of your dashboard, so you don't scroll past your own reblogs between the posts of people you follow. The rest of Tumblr still shows them, your blog and a post you open by itself included.
* New **Hide liked posts** switch on the Feed page, off by default. It takes posts you've already liked out of your dashboard, so the ones that come back around don't cost you scrolling. A post you like while it's on screen stays put until the feed loads it again. Everywhere else still shows them, so a liked post you open from a link or a notification is still there.
* A post you open by itself, from a link or a notification, now loads with Hide reblogs or Hide posts with words on. Those switches clear a feed, so they took out the very post you asked for and the page stayed empty. The same post in a feed later is still hidden as usual.
* New **Match my wallpaper** switch under Dark background color, off by default. On Android 12 or newer it takes the dark background from your wallpaper's colors, so there's no color code to pick when patching. A new wallpaper shows after Tumblr restarts.
* New **Share video links** switch on the Posts page, off by default. Long-press a video in a post and you can share its direct link or copy it. Before, a long press on a video did nothing at all. The same menu can also save the video into your Downloads folder, with a toast once it's in. Android 10 and newer don't ask for storage access for that. Android 8 and 9 ask first, and the save starts by itself once you allow it.
* New **Sharper images** switch on the Posts page, off by default. Tumblr loads photos in posts at 640 pixels across at most, and smaller on a slow connection, so they look soft on most phones. With the switch on, a photo loads from the larger copy Tumblr already keeps, up to twice the width it's shown at (usually the 1280 one), and a slow connection gets the same copy. It uses more data. GIFs go up too, but never past the pixel count of a 1280 by 960 one, since a playing GIF keeps a few frames in memory. Very large photos stay capped as well.
* New **Collapse long posts** switch on the Posts page, off by default. A long post can take several screens of scrolling. With the switch on, a post longer than about a screen gets cut in your timelines with Tumblr's own Read more row under it, the same one you see when an author cuts their post. Tap it and the whole post opens right where it is in the timeline, so you don't lose your place, and it stays open as you scroll on. That holds on tag pages too, where Tumblr fades long posts out by itself, so the post you opened isn't cut again there. A post Tumblr always cuts still opens on its own page. A post that runs only a little over stays whole. A long entry in a reblog trail gets cut the same way, and its Read more opens the post on its own page like an author's cut does. Asks keep Tumblr's layout. The **Collapsed post length** row under the switch moves the cut to about half a screen or two screens with a tap, and a settings file carries your pick.
* New **Sanitize sharing links** switch on the Privacy page, on by default. Tumblr adds `?source=share` to the post and blog links you share or copy, which tells it the visit came from a share. With the switch on, the link goes out without that tag and still opens the same post. A link is otherwise left exactly as Tumblr made it. Quick setups' Privacy choice turns it on too.
* Hide ads now takes sponsored banners out of your feeds too, the takeover banners a sponsor pays for and the Sponsored Day ones. Tumblr's own banners without a sponsor, like Answer Time, stay. When a sponsor has paid for the header at the top of Explore, that header stays empty instead, the same as when it doesn't load, so its takeover ad can't open from it.
* New **App font** patch, off by default. Turn it on under Interface and Tumblr's own font gives way to your phone's, or to a .ttf or .otf file you pick there. Fonts a post's author picked stay as they are, and a change shows after a restart.
* Flipping a switch near the bottom of a settings page doesn't make the page jump anymore, so a second tap in the same spot can't land on the switch next to the one you meant. Two things moved it. The restart row appearing above slid the list down, and while a switch saved, its shorter "Saving the setting" line let the rows above drop into the gap and stay there.
* Hide the Follow button now reaches the reblog trail too. Each blog quoted in a reblog has its own header with a Follow button, and those kept showing while the one at the top of the post was gone.
* The patch bundle is signed now. Morphe Manager shows "Signed by 0859 9c9b 1bd2 9cac" on the HushTumblr source and remembers that key, so a later bundle signed with any other key, or not signed at all, gets refused instead of loaded.
* Hide ads now also turns off the full-screen ad Tumblr can show when you open a blog, whether you get there from the dashboard, a reblog, a notification or a message. The pause Tumblr keeps between those ads is left alone, since switching it off would only take the pause away.
* When something in HushTumblr settings can't finish, what to do next now stays under the row you tapped. It used to be a toast that faded after a few seconds, and Android 12 and later show only two lines of one, so large text got cut off. This covers links no app can open, Android's app settings not opening, switches and tab moves that didn't save, a Resume or Quick setup that didn't run, font files that couldn't be used and refused exports or imports. The diagnostic report and **Clear diagnostic data** rows work the same way now, so the folder a full report went to stays readable. A link you can't open comes with a Copy link button.
* A failed tab move or import keeps what you tried, so trying again picks up where it stopped. The import preview also gets a **Choose another file** button for when the file itself was the problem. Notes under rows come back when Android rebuilds the screen (say, after you rotate the phone), and a result that lands after you've closed settings or switched apps shows under its row once you're back. One that lands while the screen is being rebuilt shows on the new one, and one for a row on another settings page waits there until you open it. Hiding Quick setups says right under that page's row where to get it back. A Tab order tap while a change is still saving opens the list once the save is done instead of doing nothing.
* A settings page opened with a keyboard or D-pad starts at the top again. The "HushTumblr is paused" line used to sit just above the view, so the switches under it read as on.
* With a keyboard, D-pad or mouse, the settings home page comes back where you left it after Back or a cleared search, even when a section heading or the status card sat at the top. It used to land one row further down, with that heading pushed out of view.
* A switch, font file or Quick setup that waits for a restart now says so right under that row. The note goes once you undo that change or nothing's waiting anymore, and a change the next start makes anyway (Pause while safe mode already pauses) doesn't get one. Screen readers read it once from there, and again only if you go back to it. The Tab order list shows "Saving changes" under it while a slow save runs, then where the tab went and whether Tumblr needs a restart, and the dialog keeps its size while those come and go. Android 16 is phasing out the pop-up announcements these used, and those were gone the moment they were spoken.
* Hide ads now keeps Tumblr's ad networks (Google Mobile Ads, Meta Audience Network, Vungle and Nimbus) from starting when Tumblr launches. They woke up at every cold start and wrote their files before any HushTumblr switch was read. Disable analytics does the same for LiveRamp's identity tracker. Turn either switch off, or pause HushTumblr, and they start again after a restart.
* With Hide hashtags on, a Tumblr TV video whose only text was its tags doesn't keep a lone "... more" under the blog name anymore. Tapping it opened nothing. A video with a description still gets its label.
* With Hide note counts on, each comment in a post's notes drops its own like and reply counts too, and TalkBack doesn't read them out either. The Reply and like buttons still work.
* New **Keep Blaze posts** option under Hide ads on the Feed page, off by default. With it on, posts someone paid to promote with Blaze stay in your dashboard while network ads and other sponsored cards still go. Your word and post type filters still apply to those posts.
* The Words to hide and Allowed words editors can test your list on a made-up post now. Type some post text, trail text, tags or a summary under **Try it on a post** and it tells you whether the word filter would hide that post. It names the word that did it and where it was, or the allowed word that kept the post. It uses the same whole-word matching and the same Match switches as your dashboard, and tags work typed with commas or the way a post shows them (#art #cats). Nothing typed there is saved, and it's empty again each time you open the editor.
* With **Mute posts locally** on, a post's menu on the dashboard can also hide one blog's reblogs or its originals. A reblog offers "Hide reblogs from" that blog and an original offers "Hide originals from" it, so you can keep someone's own posts while their reblogs go, or the other way around. The rule only works on your dashboard and its tabs. Explore, their blog page and search still show those posts, and so does a post you open by itself. It goes in the same private list as your muted posts, for this account only, and Manage muted posts shows it as its own row you can tap to remove. A post Tumblr doesn't say enough about to tell which kind it is always stays.
* Disable analytics now really keeps Crashlytics crash reports off. The patch already set Firebase's manifest flag for that, but Tumblr turns crash collection back on by itself every time it starts, and its call wins over the flag. With the switch on, that call now leaves collection off. Turn the switch off, or pause HushTumblr, and Tumblr's own setting is back after a restart.
* The README's Help explains how to keep a patched copy next to your regular Tumblr with Morphe's Clone app patch. You sign in to the copy with your email and it keeps its own HushTumblr settings, but `Fix push notifications` only covers the regular app.
* **Tooling:** The check that every GitLab mirror in a source notice still answers doesn't fail anymore when GitLab says "too many requests". It spaces its requests out, waits as long as GitLab asks (a minute at most) and tries again, so only a mirror that's really gone fails it.
* **Tooling:** The build runs on Gradle 9.8.2 with Gson 2.15.0, Guava 33.8.0 and Commons Lang 3.21.0. None of them fixes a known advisory here, so it's upkeep. The release check accepts the 9.8.2 wrapper now.
* **Tooling:** Device runs don't stop anymore when another session happens to read the shared lease file at the same moment. Opening or releasing the lease waits a few seconds and tries again, and a missing lease or one someone else holds still stops the run.
* **Tooling:** The source census reads ReVanced's GitLab mirror in three GraphQL questions now. Over REST it took a request for every branch and watched path, 70 of them for that mirror's 35 branches, and GitLab allows an address with no token about 60 an hour. Each comparison is asked once a run too. Forks that GitLab has renamed while they wait to be deleted stay out of the census, because a scanner keeps making and deleting them faster than any census could record them.
* **Tooling:** A release walk now waits for Android's verifier to finish on both the clean reference and the exact build that got installed, and both files have to hash the same before and after it runs. Publishing refuses evidence that's old or partial. It also refuses evidence that doesn't match, down to one verifier message too many or too few.
* **Tooling:** A release walk now checks each required destination instead of trusting its screen count. Login screens, wrong search results, duplicate or missing routes, recorded problems and invalid timestamps can't earn a full PASS. Shorter exploratory walks remain partial.
* **Tooling:** Release walks now prove which APK was installed. Patch runs keep the input, bundle, patch selection and signing identity beside the signed output. The walker checks the installed bytes before and after its run, and publishing refuses evidence that's missing or doesn't match this build.
* **Tooling:** `scripts/walk-app.ps1` scrolls the Hush settings list with slow drags now, down first and then back up, and stops once the list stops moving. Quick swipes flung the list past rows that slid under its pinned header, so a walk on a phone could miss settings pages that were there all along.
* **Tooling:** `scripts/patch-for-device.ps1` can now sign a gate run it reuses with the BKS key Morphe makes. Plain `apksigner.bat` can't open that keystore, so the script falls back to apksigner's own jar with BouncyCastle loaded. When both fail, the error shows what each one said.
* **Tooling:** Changelog entries don't open with a "Tumblr:" label anymore. HushTumblr only patches Tumblr, so it repeated itself on every line. The release scripts stopped adding or asking for it, and drop it from any bullet that still has one when a release is cut.
* **Tooling:** The build signs the bundle when `MORPHE_SIGNING_KEY` holds the private key, and refuses a key that isn't the one `morphe-signing.pub` publishes. Without it the build still works and says the bundle is unsigned. `release.ps1` loads the key from `HUSHTUMBLR_BUNDLE_SIGNING_KEY` for the build alone, won't build a release without it, and checks the bundle came out signed by the published key. The release push gate signs its bundle with the same key, so the two still match byte for byte. The index now carries that key as `public_key`.
* **Tooling:** Device patching and the release gate know the resource ids of Tumblr 47.0 now. Rebuilding Tumblr's resource table renames four ids and rewrites one string, just as it did on 46.8, but those sit at new ids in 47.0, so every patch run stopped at the resource check after the move.
* **Tooling:** The build uses the Morphe settings plugin 2.1.0 now, which brings Kotlin 2.4.21 with it. The old plugin brought 2.4.10, which has the Kotlin build cache flaw (CVE-2026-53914), so the build had to force Kotlin up to 2.4.20 itself. That force is gone. The new plugin also signs the bundle it builds when `MORPHE_SIGNING_KEY` is set, and the release copy is still re-signed after its timestamp is pinned, so it verifies against `morphe-signing.pub` as before.
* **Tooling:** A release that stopped partway through `gh release create` picks up where it left off. The rerun checks the draft's commit, its title and tag, and every file already uploaded, sends the rest, then publishes it with fresh notes as a full release. A draft that doesn't match, an upload another run may still be sending, or a published release with the same tag gets refused before anything on GitHub changes.

* **Tooling:** `scripts/patch-for-device.ps1` can sign with the key Morphe makes, which has no store password and its own key password. Pass `-NoStorePassword` and put the key password in `HUSHTUMBLR_SIDELOAD_KEY_PASSWORD`.

Each release now writes the README's checksum example from the signed bundle, so its label, hash and file name can't fall behind.

## 0.0.1 (2026-10-12)

* First release. HushTumblr 0.0.1 starts from HushPinterest and supports Tumblr 46.5.0.109 (version code 1460500109) on Android 8.0 or newer, with 36 patches.
* Six patches carry over and now point at Tumblr: HushTumblr settings, Disable analytics, Hide advertising ID, Remove ad tracking permissions, Fix push notifications and Spoof signature for Google sign-in.
* HushTumblr settings has a row of its own at the end of Tumblr's Settings screen, and still opens from a long-press on the Tumblr icon and from Tumblr's App info page. The row's title is the one string HushTumblr adds to Tumblr's resources.
* Patch descriptions now point at Tumblr settings > HushTumblr for each switch.
* The patches now declare all four ABIs Tumblr's 46.5.0.109 bundle carries (arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a, x86 and x86_64), so Morphe Manager offers them on 32-bit phones and x86 emulators too, not just 64-bit ARM.
* A shared hook on Tumblr's feature check lets a HushTumblr switch turn one of Tumblr's own features off. It has no switch of its own, and what it changes shows after Tumblr restarts, since Tumblr keeps each answer until then.
* New Hide ads patch, on by default. It takes ad cards and posts Tumblr serves as sponsored out of your dashboard, blog pages, likes and search before Tumblr shows them, and turns off Tumblr's switches for asking its ad networks for ads once Tumblr restarts. The switch is on the new Feed page in HushTumblr settings.
* Disable analytics now has a switch, on by default, on the Privacy page. It turns off Tumblr's own tracking once Tumblr restarts: LiveRamp's ID on its requests, Open Measurement and Moat ad measurement, and its logs of which features you saw, network use, app performance and ad supply. The Firebase and Google part still stays until you patch again without it.
* New Hide the ad-free banner patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Feed page, hides the dashboard banner that asks you to pay for Ad-Free Browsing once Tumblr restarts.
* New Hide Tumblr TV patch. Its switch, off to start and on the new Interface page, takes the Tumblr TV tab out of the bottom bar and stops sign-up from sending you there, once Tumblr restarts.
* New Hide the Communities tab patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Interface page, takes the Communities tab out of the bottom bar and stops the Communities tips in the post editor, once Tumblr restarts.
* New Hide Blaze prompts patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Interface page, hides the buttons, banners and after-post nudge selling Blaze, Tumblr's paid post promotion, once Tumblr restarts.
* New Skip review prompts patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Interface page, stops the "What do you think of the Tumblr app?" pop-up that leads to a store rating or a feedback form. It's read each time the pop-up would open, so it works without a restart.
* New Turn off screenshot sharing patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Interface page, stops Tumblr watching for screenshots and opening its offer to save the post as an image, once Tumblr restarts.
* New Disable update prompts patch. Its switch, off to start and first on the Updates page under More settings, stops Tumblr asking Google Play for a newer Tumblr and showing Play's update prompt, once Tumblr restarts.
* New Hide suggested blogs, Hide suggested communities and Hide Watch it rows patches. Their switches, off to start and on the Feed page, take the "Check out these blogs" and "Check out these communities" cards and Tumblr TV's Watch it rows out of your dashboard and other feeds as each page loads. A carousel goes when its first card is one of them, and the heading in front of a card goes with it.
* New Hide hashtags and Hide the Follow button patches, with their switches off to start on a new Posts page in HushTumblr settings. Hide hashtags takes the tags off the bottom of posts and keeps the source link and badges in that row, and clears the tags Tumblr TV writes under a video that has no description. Hide the Follow button takes the button out of the header of posts from blogs you don't follow yet. Both apply to the next post that scrolls in, without a restart.
* New Hide note counts, Hide the like button, Hide the reblog button, Hide the comment button and Hide the share button patches, with their switches off to start on the Posts page. Hide note counts takes the like, reblog and comment counts off the footer under posts and keeps the buttons working, and shows on the next post that scrolls in. Where Tumblr writes the count as words, on a community post's comment button and at the top of a post's notes, you get Tumblr's own "Comments" or "Notes" instead. Each button switch takes that one button out of the footer once Tumblr restarts. Community posts have their own footer, and the comment and share switches reach it too. They have no like button, so Hide the like button takes their reactions off instead.
* New Hide the diamond and TumblrMart patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Interface page, hides the Premium diamond and the TumblrMart button at the top of your dashboard, Explore and notifications once Tumblr restarts. Explore's search bar then runs on into the space the two icons left. Premium itself isn't touched, so checkout and perks keep working for members.
* New Hide unread dots patch. It puts three switches on the Interface page, all off to start: Hide the Dashboard count, Hide the Communities count and Hide the Notifications count. Each one hides the unread dot or count on its own tab of the bottom bar the next time Tumblr updates its counts, so you can keep the one you care about.
* New Hide reblogs patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Feed page, takes reblogs out of your dashboard and other feeds as each page loads and keeps the posts people made themselves.
* New Hide video posts patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Feed page, takes posts with a video anywhere in them out of your feeds as each page loads. The reblogged part counts, so a comment reblogged onto a video is a video post too.
* New Hide photo posts patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Feed page, takes posts with pictures and no video out of your feeds as each page loads, the reblogged part included.
* New Hide text posts patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Feed page, takes posts with no pictures or video out of your feeds as each page loads, the reblogged part included.
* New Hide posts with these words patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Feed page, takes a post out of your feeds when its text, reblog trail, tags or summary has a word or phrase from the list you type in under it. Case doesn't matter and only whole words count.
* New Dark background color patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Interface page, makes the background of Tumblr's Dark Mode palette black once Tumblr restarts, which can save battery on OLED screens. The patch's Background color option picks another dark color instead. Cards get the same color and highlighted parts a shade lighter, and the other palettes, light ones included, stay as they are. The System palette gets it at night, when it uses Dark Mode.
* New Hide the Explore tab patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Interface page, takes the Explore and search button off the bottom bar once Tumblr restarts. The tab itself stays, so a link into Explore still opens it.
* New Hide the Notifications tab patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Interface page, takes the Notifications button off the bottom bar once Tumblr restarts. A link into your notifications still opens them.
* New Reorder the bottom bar patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Interface page, puts the bottom bar's tabs in the order you set in the Tab order list under it, once Tumblr restarts. A tap on a tab in that list moves it up a place, and Tumblr's order puts them back. Tumblr still opens on Dashboard, and each button and each link into a tab still opens its own tab. Tumblr TV and Communities are skipped while their own switches keep them off the bar, and Dashboard and Account always stay.
* New Hide the Save button patch. Its switch, off to start and on the Posts page, takes the Save button out of the footer under posts once Tumblr restarts. Tumblr only draws that button when its own Save feature is on for you, so without it there's nothing to hide.
* Hide note counts now takes the number off each reaction on community posts too, and leaves the emoji.
* Export settings now saves your words to hide and your tab order along with the switches, and Import settings brings them back. A settings file from before this still imports its switches and leaves both as they are. If a file's word list or tab order can't be used, the import preview says so and leaves it out, and the rest of the file still imports.
* The switches that answer Tumblr's own feature flags (Disable analytics, Hide the ad-free banner, Hide Tumblr TV, Hide the Communities tab, Hide Blaze prompts, Turn off screenshot sharing and Disable update prompts) now act like the other switches that need a restart. Changing one puts "A change here applies after Tumblr restarts." at the top of its page and shows the restart toast, so their own descriptions no longer end in "Restart Tumblr to see the change."
* The diagnostics report now counts any Tumblr feature check a HushTumblr switch claims that runs before HushTumblr has started, since Tumblr keeps that first answer until it restarts. Reading Tumblr 46.5.0.109 found none.
* Fix push notifications hands Firebase Tumblr's original certificate fingerprint in its request builder, and Hide advertising ID answers Tumblr's ad ID lookups with an empty ID.
