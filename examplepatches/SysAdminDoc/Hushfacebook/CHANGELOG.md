# Changelog

Every Hushfacebook release, newest first.

## 0.5.0 (2026-09-29)

* **Facebook:** This release gathers everything since v0.4.0: 51 patches for Facebook 580 and 577, up from 43. The eight new ones are `Hide the Reels tab dot`, `Hide the Reels tab`, `Keep the reel speed`, `Hold a reel for 2x`, `Default playback quality`, `Turn off double tap to like`, `Open Messenger from the top bar` and `View stories anonymously`. `Hide the Reels tab dot`, `Keep the reel speed` and `Open Messenger from the top bar` are in Morphe Manager's default selection, the last with its switch off until you turn it on, and the other five are yours to pick. Among the fixes, the "Suggested for you" groups row is hidden again, Marketplace search results lose their ads, stories and VP9 reels save at full quality, AMOLED cards turn black, and a Messenger patched with the same key signs in through your Facebook.
* **Tooling:** The build notes now say the unit tests on Windows need JDK 25 or newer, and the test run warns on an older one, since those JDKs can't rename a file over an existing one there. Two test classes also stopped leaking state into the ones after them, which had made a translation test and a launcher shortcut test fail now and then.
* **Facebook:** `Hide the Reels tab` now takes Facebook's Reels shortcut out of the long-press menu of its icon too, so that way into Reels goes with the tab. Checked on a device: the Reels shortcut was gone after a start with the switch on, and Facebook put it back at the next start with the switch off.
* **Facebook:** New patch, `Hide the Reels tab dot`, on by default under Reels and Watch. The Reels tab, which some accounts call Video, stops showing a dot or a number for new reels, and every other tab keeps its count. On a phone the Reels tab's dot and its "1 new" went, and Notifications kept its badge.
* **Facebook:** The "Suggested for you" row of groups to join, with its Discover more groups button, is hidden again. Facebook now sends it as one of its own promo cards instead of the groups row the feed filter knew, so it got through even with every switch on. Hide suggested groups, or Hide page suggestions and Facebook's own promos, now takes it out, along with Facebook's other cards of that kind. Checked on a phone: the row sat at the top of Home with both switches off and was gone with them on.
* **Facebook:** If CapCut or InShot won't open a saved video, or can't export it, the answer is now in settings: the Save videos other apps can open switch under Downloads says it's for video editors such as CapCut and InShot too. By default a save keeps Facebook's sharpest picture, which can be AV1 video, and some stories come with xHE-AAC sound only. In a test on a phone, CapCut opened AV1 video as an empty black clip, and xHE-AAC sound left CapCut's export stuck near the end and made InShot's export silent. H.264 or VP9 video with AAC sound opened and exported fine in both. With the switch on, a save keeps to H.264 video with AAC sound, or Facebook's own MP4 when there's no such pair. Checked on a phone: with the switch on, a reel whose manifest offered only AV1 video and xHE-AAC sound saved as Facebook's own 720p H.264 MP4 with HE-AAC sound, and CapCut and InShot both opened it and exported it with its sound.
* **Facebook:** New patch, `Default playback quality`, for anyone who wants to save data or always get the sharpest picture. Pick Data saver, Up to 480p, Up to 720p or Highest under Playback in Hushfacebook's settings, and reels, Watch videos, feed videos and video stories play at that quality where Facebook offers it. A video with nothing at or under your pick plays the closest quality above. Facebook's own quality menu changes only the video you pick in, so the next reel goes back to Auto, and a pick there still wins for that video. It's off in Morphe Manager, and once it's in, the list starts at Auto, Facebook's own choice, so nothing changes until you pick one. Settings files carry the choice too. Checked on a phone: with Up to 480p a new reel started at 480p, and with Highest at 1080p.
* **Facebook:** New patch, `Hold a reel for 2x`. Press and hold a playing reel and it plays at double speed until you let go, then goes back to the speed it had, even when that's a speed you picked in the menu or one `Keep the reel speed` carried over. Pull your finger down while you hold and Facebook locks the reel at double speed, with a "Locked at 2x speed" note, until you move on to the next reel. Facebook already built this into its Reels player for some accounts, and the patch turns it on anywhere on the reel. The hold takes the place of Facebook's long-press menu on reels, which the reel's more button still opens, and ads keep that menu. A tap still plays or pauses, and double taps and the side buttons work as before. The patch starts off in Morphe Manager, and its switch, under Reels and Watch, starts on once you pick it. Checked on a phone: on a reel playing at a kept 2x, a two-second hold and a half-second hold each went to 2x, and letting go put 2x back.
* **Facebook:** New patch, `Keep the reel speed`, for anyone tired of picking the same playback speed on every reel (issue #25). Pick a speed in a reel's menu and the reels after it play at that speed too, until you pick another one or Facebook restarts. Picking Normal brings back Facebook's own way in that place only, where every new reel starts at normal speed. A reel you pause and start again stays at whatever speed it's at. The speed sticks to the place you picked it, so a speed picked in Reels carries on through Reels, whether your account has them in their own tab or in the Video tab, and a speed picked on a reel in your feed carries on through the feed's reels. Ads and live videos play at Facebook's own speed, and so does anything that isn't a reel. The patch is in the default selection and its switch is under Reels and Watch. Checked on a phone: after picking 2x, each next reel played at 2x once it started.
* **Facebook:** Stories and reels that Facebook sends only as VP9 video now save at full quality with their sound, instead of dropping to the story's 360p file with a "lower quality" message. Android's own MP4 writer won't take VP9, so Hushfacebook now writes that MP4 itself: it copies the VP9 picture and the AAC sound into one file as they are, without re-encoding, and keeps them in sync. The same writer handles AV1 before Android 14, so a phone on Android 11 to 13 that can play AV1 now saves AV1 stories and reels at full quality too. Formats every app plays, like H.264, still go through Android's writer, and with `Save videos other apps can open` on, H.264 is still what a save picks. Some players and editors don't open VP9, so turn that switch on if one turns a save down. Checked on a phone: a VP9-only story saved at 1080p and played with its sound in the phone's own video player.
* **Facebook:** With `AMOLED black theme` on in dark mode, cards no longer stay dark grey on the black page (issue #27). Facebook's dark mode draws cards a shade lighter than the page, #333334, and AMOLED only turned the page black, so things like a Page's card in search results or the Reel and Live bar on a profile stood out as grey blocks. Cards and comment bubbles now go near black, about #121212, and pop-up menus a touch lighter. They stay just off black so a card still shows its edge, and a message box or button drawn on one still stands out from it. A search field or a pill on a black page used the same fill as a card, so the first pass took it down just as far and left its shape barely visible. It now only goes down partway, to about #262627, enough to still show against black. The Page card in search results needed its own fix, since Facebook sends the colours for search results from its servers instead of taking them from the app. That card, with its Follow button and "Write a message..." box, now gets the same near black, and the message box stays a visible step lighter than the card. Colours from the server that are a bit lighter than a card, like some buttons and dividers, keep Facebook's grey. Light mode doesn't change. Checked on a phone on a Page's card in search results.
* **Facebook:** `Hide sponsored Marketplace listings` now takes the ads out of Marketplace search results too (issue #29). A search gets its ads back mixed in with the listings, so they come out of the answer before Marketplace draws it, and the listings close up with no empty tile. The typeahead suggestions and your search history stay as they were. The diagnostic report counts what came out on its `Marketplace search ads` line. Checked on a phone: a search for bikes, scrolled through several pages, showed no ad tile and no gap, and a listing and its Message seller screen opened as usual.
* **Facebook:** A new `Hide the Reels tab` patch takes the Reels tab, which some accounts call Video, off the tab bar (issue #26). It works whether or not your account gets Facebook's own Hide under Settings, Tab bar, Customize the bar, and it never undoes that Hide, so a tab you hid there stays hidden with the switch off too. Reel links, the reels in your feed and the Reels viewer still open. Home, Marketplace, Notifications and Menu keep their order, just moved one slot to where Reels used to be. Its switch is under Reels and Watch and starts on once you pick the patch in Morphe Manager. Turning it off, or pausing Hushfacebook, brings the tab back the next time Facebook starts. If Open on a chosen tab is set to Video, Facebook opens on Home while the tab is hidden, and the Tab to open on row says so. Checked on a phone: the tab was gone at a cold start with Facebook's own Hide option set to Auto. Turning the switch off and restarting brought it back, and with Video set as the tab to open on, Facebook opened on Home.
* **Facebook:** Saved stories now come out at the quality the story plays at, up to your Download quality, even when you save the moment a story opens or turned on `Save any story` while it was open. The save used to look for the story's player to find its better tracks, and when it hadn't seen that player yet it fell back to the story's own 360p file without saying so. A story carries the same list of tracks its player uses, so the save now reads it from the story itself. And when a save ends up clearly below the best picture Facebook shows, for example when a save has to fall back to a smaller file, the message after the save says it's lower quality than on Facebook, measured from the file that was actually saved rather than guessed from its address. The diagnostic report names the best picture on offer whenever the saved file measures below it, not a guess read off the address it fetched. A small shortfall, like a 720p save when the only bigger track is AV1 and the phone can't write AV1 yet, doesn't raise that message on its own, but the same shortfall against a picture the phone could actually have written, such as a dropped DASH fetch that falls back to a smaller H.264 file, still does. That message was also too quick to show up on a plain, working save: a file the phone couldn't measure back, a couple of pixels of rounding, and a track your Download quality setting wouldn't actually have used could all trigger it on their own. It now only shows up for a real shortfall against a picture your setting would have let through. Checked on a phone: a story saved from its menu came out at 1080p from the story's own track list.
* **Facebook:** A Messenger, Messenger Lite or Facebook Lite you patch with the same key as your Facebook is now treated as Meta's own when it calls your Facebook, so it gets exactly what the Meta-signed app would and can sign in through it. Facebook checks a caller's signing certificate against its own in a few different ways, so a re-signed Messenger was turned away with a security error even though you signed both with your one Manager key, and widening one of those checks alone didn't cover the rest. Now, when one of them calls in carrying your key, Facebook reads its certificate as Meta's, the same certificate a re-signed Facebook already reads for itself, so every check passes just as it does for the real thing. Those are the Meta apps signed with Facebook's own certificate. Instagram and Instagram Lite carry Instagram's, so they keep Facebook's own answer like any other app, and so does one whose name only looks like a Meta app's, one signed with another key, or a caller that can't be identified. A Root Mount install keeps Meta's key, so nothing changes there. This comes with `Restore screens on re-signed builds`, whether or not you also pick `Install beside Meta's apps`. To check it on a phone, turn on Debug logging, open Hushfacebook settings again and tap **Test the Messenger link** under Pause, backup and diagnostics: it runs the two reads Facebook makes through Messenger at startup right away, and says whether each one answered without showing what it read. The test was checked on a phone: with a Messenger patched with the same key, both reads answered and Messenger let Facebook in. The other way round works too: a fresh Messenger patched with the same key listed the Facebook accounts under Continue as and signed in with a tap, no password.
* **Facebook:** New patch, `Turn off double tap to like`, for anyone who keeps liking reels by accident. With its switch on, a double tap on a reel or a video doesn't like it and the heart doesn't show, in Reels, Watch, the video viewer and the reels Facebook chains from a post in the feed. A single tap still plays or pauses, and the Like button still likes. The patch starts off in Morphe Manager, and its switch, under Reels and Watch in Hushfacebook's settings, starts on once you pick it. Checked on a phone on the Reels tab.
* **Facebook:** The diagnostic report now says why `Block background-return feed refresh` let Facebook refresh, such as away over ten minutes or the switch being off, next to how often it kept the feed instead. Before, the report's line for it only said how many times a check ran, and a feed resume or tab switch that wasn't a background return at all counted the same as a real refusal, so a working install's report could read "let Facebook refresh" a dozen times over with nothing wrong. Checked on a phone: after a 7-minute return the report counted the feed kept, and after an 11-minute one it counted "let Facebook refresh: away over ten minutes".
* **Facebook:** With Facebook's dark mode off, the Video tab's bottom bar keeps Facebook's own dark grey under `AMOLED black theme` and `Material You theme`. That tab stays dark in light mode and reads the same colours dark mode does, so AMOLED turned the bar black and Material You could tint the tab's other dark surfaces. Both themes now ask Facebook whether its dark mode is on before they change a colour that either mode could use. In dark mode the bar still goes black with AMOLED, and with Material You alone it now takes your palette like the bar on the other tabs, as do other screens that read Facebook's dark grey the same way. Checked on a phone with both themes.
* **Facebook:** With Facebook in light mode, the Video tab's status bar and navigation bar keep Facebook's own colours under `AMOLED black theme` and `Material You theme`. That tab stays dark in light mode, and Facebook writes its dark grey bar colour straight into its code for both themes, so the themes turned the bars black or tinted them even with dark mode off. That colour is now left for the bar painters, which check whether Facebook's dark mode is on before they change anything. In dark mode the bars still go black, or take your palette with Material You alone. Checked on a phone with both themes, before and after Recent Apps.
* **Facebook:** A word list saved right as you close the settings page now takes effect straight away instead of waiting for Facebook to restart. The page could stop listening for that save a moment before it actually landed, so the word filter kept running the old list until a restart caught up. Not yet checked on a phone.
* **Facebook:** Pop-ups the settings page opens itself, like Licenses or the note about word list lines that were left out, now close when the page goes away, for example when you leave settings. Android counted them as leaked windows before. The note also no longer tries to open over a page that's already closing. Not yet checked on a phone.
* **Facebook:** Clone app's coexistence fix from issue #16 only catches the package rename when a selected patch whose name sorts before "Clone app" is picked too, and choosing Clone app alone, or only with patches named after it, skipped that fix without a word about why. The patch log now spells out, at its detailed level, what to add, such as Block ad telemetry, which is on by default. Checked with unit tests that reproduce the selection, not yet on a phone.
* **Facebook:** On Android, the diagnostic report now leaves out the whole of a value or web address that has a no-break space in it. Android's regex engine took that space for the end of the value, so the part after it stayed in a report you share. A name starting with the old long s, as in ſecret=, loses its value now too. So that a phone reads every name the way the tests do, the report writes ß, the long s, the Kelvin sign and letter ligatures like ﬁ out in plain letters, which means ß shows as ss. Checked with ICU, the regex library Android builds on, but not yet on a phone.
* **Facebook:** A copy made with Morphe's Clone app no longer crashes as it opens when the Facebook it was cloned from is installed too (issue #16). Facebook's code reaches some of its own content providers by the name com.facebook.katana, so the copy was reaching into the original app, which turned it away. Those names now follow the copy's package. With Clone app's Update permissions option on, the copy's manifest also went on requiring permissions only the original declares, and now it names the copy's own. With Update providers off, the copy's providers get its own names too, so it installs beside the original instead of clashing with it. A Facebook that isn't cloned doesn't change. Checked on a phone: a copy made with Update permissions on, and one with it off, both open to Facebook's sign-in screen and stay open, and Hushfacebook's settings open in them. Screens that need you signed in to the copy haven't been tried yet.
* **Tooling:** The tests for saves that join a video's picture and its sound now go through the same save a tap on a phone starts. They called shortcut forms of the join that nothing on a phone uses, so a fault in how a real save hands over its progress or its size limit could slip past them. The shortcuts are gone, and every check those tests made still runs on the real path.
* **Facebook:** When you save a word list under `Hide posts by words` and some lines are left out, the explanation now opens in a small window you close with OK. It used to be a pop-up message, and Android 12 and later cut those off after two lines, so most of the reasons went unseen, even more so with large text. The wording hasn't changed. Checked on a phone at normal and double text size.
* **Facebook:** Saves already take the ordinary AAC sound track when Facebook offers one. Some videos come with xHE-AAC sound only, though, and the save keeps it rather than make a silent file. Some players can't play xHE-AAC, so the diagnostic report now says so on that save's line. If a saved video plays without sound, try turning on Save videos other apps can open. For a video like that it saves Facebook's own MP4 instead when there is one. Checked on a phone: a 1080p reel that only had xHE-AAC sound saved with the note, and it played with sound in Samsung Gallery.
* **Facebook:** `Block background-return feed refresh` now holds the feed for its whole window, ten minutes, or any time away with No time limit on (issue #23). After a few minutes in the background Facebook clears the feed it has loaded, and when you come back it checks whether the feed is stale, so after about six minutes away you still landed on new posts. Only one of Facebook's checks was held before. With the switch on, the feed now stays loaded while you're away, and every check Facebook makes as you come back keeps your place, including the auto-scroll some accounts get. A return past ten minutes without No time limit still loads new posts, and so do pull to refresh and a fresh start. With Debug logging on, the log says what each check decided. Checked on a phone: after 7 minutes away, and after 11 with No time limit on, the feed was where you left it, and 11 minutes without No time limit brought new posts.
* **Facebook:** On Android, the diagnostic report now also drops a token, password, cookie, ID, web address or @handle written straight after a letter like é, я or 猫, as in étoken=... Android's regex engine counts those letters as part of the word, so it found no name there and kept the value, while the tests, which run on a computer, saw it go. An @ in between doesn't hide anything either, so in é@token=... or josé@facebook.com/... the handle goes along with the value or the address after it, and the same is true with nothing unusual in front of the @ at all: @token=..., @access_token=..., @c_user=... and @user_id=... now lose their value too, while an ordinary handle like @dana or @facebook still just goes. Checked with ICU, the regex library Android builds on, but not yet on a phone.
* **Facebook:** The diagnostic report keeps the value after a few ordinary words that happen to contain sid, uid, guid or auth, such as inside, guide, residual, author and authority. Lines like "inside: 3 rows", "guide=on" or "author: 3f9a2c1b0e7d" used to lose everything after the colon or equals sign. Any other name holding one of those short names still has its value left out, including run-together ones like authkey, basicauth, SAPISID or FBUID, so a name the report doesn't know stays hidden. Plain forms of those same ordinary words the list missed, such as authorized, unauthorized, authorize, considering and reconsider, kept their value too and now do the same. Not yet checked on a phone.
* **Facebook:** When a save can't show its notification, because Facebook's notifications or the Hushfacebook saves channel are off, the message as it starts now says where to cancel it: "Saving... Cancel: Downloads in Hushfacebook." It stays up a little longer so there's time to read it, and the wording is short enough that Android's largest font size still shows the whole thing rather than cutting it off after two lines. With the notification showing, a save still just says "Saving...". Translated into all five languages, though a translation can run longer than English and may still be cut at that font size. Checked on a phone at double text size in English.
* **Facebook:** On Android 15 and newer the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen now turns black with the AMOLED theme. Facebook draws that bar itself there, and it came up in Facebook's own dark grey until a tab asked for another colour. Other parts of the app that ask Facebook's theme for a colour the same way, like parts of the top bar, now pick up the theme too. Light mode doesn't change. Checked on a phone with AMOLED. With Material You alone in dark mode the bottom strip now takes your palette too, the same colour as the tab bar above it. Facebook colours that strip for every tab with the Video tab's dark grey, and Material You now tints that grey the same way it tints the Video tab's own bar. Checked on a phone with Material You alone too.
* **Facebook:** If an import fails after you press Import but before the settings are written, Export settings and Import settings now come back at once with "Couldn't start that. Try again in a moment." Before, the two rows could stay greyed out until Facebook restarted. Nothing in the current build is known to fail there, so this only guards against a future bug. Checked with unit tests, not on a phone.
* **Facebook:** The diagnostic report now leaves out an account ID written after user_id, userid, userId, user-id or their plurals, whatever it looks like. Only an ID of 15 digits or more was caught before, so a shorter or non-numeric one could reach a report you share. A list of account IDs goes whole, written with commas or spaces or as JSON, and words after the ID on its line go with it, up to the next name with an equals sign or colon, since they can't be told from a second ID. The value of a HAR-style name and value pair naming an account, post, story or other ID is left out as well, and so is a list or object under any ID name. Not yet checked on a phone.
* **Facebook:** With the Material You theme in dark mode, the status bar on Android 15 and newer now takes your wallpaper palette, both when Facebook opens and after you come back from Recent Apps. Facebook paints that bar itself on newer Android, and it kept Facebook's own greys. With AMOLED in the same build the bar stays black, and light mode doesn't change. Checked on a phone.
* **Facebook:** New patch, `Open Messenger from the top bar` ([#19](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/Hushfacebook/issues/19)). With its switch on, a tap on the Messenger icon at the top of Facebook opens the Messenger app instead of Facebook's own Chats. It starts Messenger the way its home screen icon does, so the Messenger from the Play Store works beside a patched Facebook. Without Messenger installed, Chats opens as before. A long press on the icon still does what Facebook does with it, also on phones where Facebook takes a held press as a plain tap once you let go. The patch is in the default selection with its switch off, so turn it on under Chats in Hushfacebook's settings. A tap opening Messenger and a long press staying in Facebook have both been checked on a phone.
* **Facebook:** New patch, `View stories anonymously` ([#24](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/Hushfacebook/issues/24)). Facebook adds you to a story's viewer list when the story viewer reports which stories you watched, and the patch stops that report before it's even built. Replying or reacting still shows you, since those are sent separately. Because Facebook never hears about the view, a story you watched keeps its unwatched ring, and it stays that way after Facebook restarts. The patch starts off in Morphe Manager, and its switch, under Stories in Hushfacebook's settings, starts on once you pick it. On a phone, stories watched with the switch on were still listed as unwatched when Facebook reloaded the tray, while one watched with it off was marked as seen. Nobody has looked at the viewer list from the other side yet.
* **Facebook:** `Hide posts by words` now takes a single character when it's a word on its own: a Chinese character like 猫, a kana, a Hangul syllable or an emoji. Before, every phrase needed at least two characters, so none of these could be listed. A lone letter or digit is still left out because it would hide nearly every post. The dialogs and the message about left-out lines now say which lengths work, in all five languages. Checked with unit tests, not yet on a phone.
* **Facebook:** The Morphe Manager descriptions of `Marketplace only` and `Open on a chosen tab` now say their switches start off. Marketplace only is in the default selection, so its description read as if patching alone would hide your tabs. Turn either on under Opening Facebook in Hushfacebook's settings.
* **Facebook:** `Hide sponsored reels` can no longer pass an error into Facebook's Reels page. If a page can't be read while it's being filtered, it now goes through as Facebook sent it and the diagnostic report names the hook that failed. The section filter already worked this way. Found in a code audit, not reported by anyone.
* **Facebook:** `Hide AI-detected reels and videos` now reads both of the signals Facebook's own Reels menu uses to offer its "AI info" row: the reel's AI attribution and the detection result on the reel itself. Before, reels on the Reels tab were checked only for the second and other reels only for the first, so a reel Facebook had flagged one way could stay. It still hides only what Facebook's detection marked, never a reel only its creator labelled. It hasn't been checked on a flagged reel yet.
* **Facebook:** `Open links in external browser` now sends a link out even when its address starts with HTTPS:// or HTTP:// in capitals. Those used to stay in Facebook's own browser. Links now go only to apps that say they open web pages, as Android recommends for any link one app hands another.
* **Facebook:** Three settings fixes from a design pass. With the Material You theme in dark mode, a switch that's off now has a dark thumb that stands out from its grey track; the light thumb there was too faint to read at a glance. The filled button in a dialog, such as Import or Save, keeps some space from the dialog's bottom edge. The message on the page shown when settings can't open is centred under its title.
* **Facebook:** Brazilian Portuguese reads more naturally, thanks to @ViictorNunes (PR #15). Their fixes are in every row the settings still show, and rows added since then now use the same words, like publicação for post and aplicativo for app. A few slips were corrected along the way, such as "um publicação" and "patrocinadass". Checked in offscreen renders at normal and double text size, not on a phone.
* **Facebook:** The status card on the settings overview, and the paused line on each category page, put their Pause, Resume or Undo button under the text once the text size is 150% or more. Beside the text it left so little room that "Hushfacebook" broke in the middle of the word. While paused, the card now says to tap Resume and then restart Facebook, where it used to read as if the restart came first.
* **Tooling:** The download tests now go through the same save a tap on a phone starts. Several of them used an older all-in-one save that nothing on a phone runs anymore, so they could pass while the real save did something else. That old save is gone, and so are the shortcut forms of the fetch and the gallery copy that only tests called. Every check those tests made still runs, now on the real path.
* **Tooling:** When a settings backup test fails while its stand-in file app still holds a file open, the tests after it now start clean. The cleanup used to time out waiting for that file before it reset anything, so every later test found the Export and Import rows still busy and failed as well, which hid the one that broke. It now lets the file go first.
* **Tooling:** The two settings backup tests where a file app stops answering now wait until the stand-in app is really holding the file before they let the 30 seconds run out. On a busy machine the timeout could fire before the file was even opened, and the check that the cancel reached the app failed now and then.
* **Facebook:** Cancel now stops a save at once when you press it just as the save starts downloading. At that moment the Cancel could reach the connection before it had opened, so it closed nothing, and the save went on waiting for the server's answer for up to 20 seconds. Its notification disappeared as soon as you pressed Cancel, but the "Save cancelled" message came late and the save kept one of the three save slots until then. Checked on a phone: a Cancel pressed a quarter of a second after the tap stopped the save right away.
* **Tooling:** The test that cancels one of two running saves no longer fails on a busy machine. It gave the cancelled save 15 seconds to stop. Now both downloads are let through to the end and the test checks how each save ended, so a Cancel that doesn't work still fails it.

## 0.4.0 (2026-09-28)

* **Facebook:** This release gathers everything since v0.3.4, still 43 patches for Facebook 580 and 577. A copy of Facebook renamed with Morphe's Clone app patch can open its Settings screens again (issue #16). The AMOLED status bar stays black after you come back from Recent Apps (issue #22), and Block background-return feed refresh can now keep your place with no time limit (issue #23). Saves got the most work. Running saves now show under Downloads with a Cancel button, and a save cut short is cleaned up the next time Facebook starts. Error pages and oversized files also stop a save sooner. The diagnostic report always has your build details now, leaves out more private values and says why a reel has no Download button (issue #18). Most of this was checked with tests and has not been tried on a phone yet, so please report anything that looks off.
* **Facebook:** `Restore screens on re-signed builds` now works in a copy renamed by Morphe's Clone app patch (issue #16). It used to recognize itself only by the name com.facebook.katana. Now it compares the ID Android gives each installed app, so a clone counts as itself while stock Facebook installed beside it, Messenger and look-alike names keep their own signatures. Isolated helper processes, like the in-app browser's renderers, run under an ID of their own, so they keep the old name check. Dark mode and Active status should open in a clone again, but that hasn't been checked on a phone yet. Nor have sign-in and notifications in a clone.
* **Facebook:** A save now stops before copying anything when it can't write down its unfinished gallery entry. That record is how a save cut short by Android gets cleaned up later, so an entry missing from it could sit half written in the gallery for about a week.
