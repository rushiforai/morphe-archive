# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Changed and fixed

- Gallery selections are checked off the UI thread before returning to the chat. A canceled, revoked or timed-out selection returns no media, and Report a problem includes a fixed gallery result category without file paths. Refs #94.
- Patching now reports when discovery stages start and finish. The Wi-Fi scan reads method signatures directly instead of formatting every reference in Messenger, which cuts that scan's time and temporary allocations. Refs #93, #99, #100.
- **Hide Meta AI** now reaches the search screen too. Tap the search bar and the box at the top used to say "Ask Meta AI or search" even with the switch on. Now it shows the plain search hint, like the bar on the Chats list. Search still finds people and chats the same way.
- With **Slide chats in and out** on, a chat whose first frames come late no longer jumps partway in. That happened most on the first chat you open after starting Messenger. The chat now waits off screen until Messenger draws on time (a quarter second at most), then slides in from the edge. Going back works the same way.

## 0.26.0 (2026-10-22)

### Reported issues

- [#69](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/69) New **Quiet incoming calls** switch under Conversations. Calls still come in, but Messenger doesn't ring, vibrate or pop the call up over what you're doing. The call waits in the notification shade with its Answer and Decline buttons, and a call you miss still shows up in the chat. If the Facebook app is on your phone, it can still ring for the same call. It starts off.
- [#81](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/81) New **Hide tab badges** switch under Navigation. It clears the red dots and counts from the tabs at the bottom and from the Facebook button at the top. Want one of them back, say the Chats count? Turn on its Keep switch right below. Restart Messenger after a change. It's off until you turn it on.
- [#85](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/85) New **Hide the recent photo card** switch under Photos and videos. Take a screenshot or a photo and Messenger puts a card above the message box offering to send it. With this on, the card doesn't show up. Anything you took is still in the gallery button when you want to send it. It starts off.
- [#64](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/64) With two gallery apps on the phone and neither set as the default, **Open your gallery app** sent you to a Samsung Gallery that listed photos only. Videos show up there now too, and Android's list of apps to pick from still holds just your galleries.
- [#64](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/64) and [#82](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/82) **Open your gallery app** lets you pick several photos and videos at once. Pick two or more and Messenger's Send to sheet opens with all of them and the chat you came from at the top, so one Send gets them there. Pick just one and it opens in Messenger's editor like before. Samsung's Gallery now asks for a tap on Done even when you pick a single photo.
- [#80](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/80) **Stop analytics uploads** now also stops the upload Messenger 584 starts from inside the app. On 584 Messenger can send its analytics batch, the one that holds the tap and scroll details from the report, without going through the background upload services the switch already stopped. With the switch on, that one doesn't start either.
- [#79](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/79) An auto reply can send a picture too. Open a reply under **Auto reply** and tap **Add a picture**. The text goes out first and the picture follows a couple of seconds later in the same chat, with the same once-per-chat wait, Off and Pause as the text. HushMessenger keeps its own copy of the picture, so moving or deleting the original won't break the reply. If that copy is gone, or a Messenger update changed how photos are sent, the text still goes out on its own.

### Changed and fixed

- HushMessenger now patches Messenger 584.0.0.49.98 (version code 346814894) and no longer takes 583.0.0.29.95. That's the one arm64 build of 584 on [APKMirror](https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/facebook-2/messenger/facebook-messenger-584-0-0-49-98-release/) so far. Every switch was checked against it again, so update Messenger to 584 before you repatch.
- **Hide inbox ads** is gone. Meta stopped selling Messenger inbox ads in November 2025, and Messenger 584 took out the ad cards the switch used to filter, so there's nothing left for it to hide. If you pick patches by hand in Morphe, it just won't be in the list anymore.
- The six photo and video switches now have their own **Photos and videos** group, in Morphe's patch list and on the settings Controls tab alike: original quality photos and videos, **Fix videos that won't send**, the camera and gallery app switches, and **Hide the recent photo card**. Conversations had grown too long to scan. The Chats filter still shows them.
- **Patch controls** and **Restart Messenger** now stay at the top of the icon's long-press menu while Messenger sits in the background. New messages used to push them below the four rows your launcher shows until you next opened and left Messenger. Android 9 and 10 still put them back only when Messenger opens or closes.
- Hiding Quick setups with a long press now names the button that brings it back, **Quick setups section** under Your choices. The old message sent you to the App tab you were already on, and the button talked about a home page that doesn't exist.
- A video picked in a chat whose access ran out before or during the send no longer gets **Fix videos that won't send** marked as failing in settings. It goes out the way Messenger would send it anyway.
- If the phone's sound encoder won't start while a video's sound is being fixed, it's let go right away instead of staying held until Messenger closes.
- The copies **Fix videos that won't send** makes of videos you pick now go a day after their last use, even if you never pick another video or you've turned the switch off. Messenger clears them when it starts and again while it's open as each one's day runs out. They used to sit in Messenger's cache until the next pick.
- Android 12 and newer show only two lines of a pop-up message and cut the rest with "...", which hid the useful part of 27 of HushMessenger's. They're shorter now and fit, from the font file and backup messages to the camera, gallery and restart ones.
- Restoring your choices pops up how many of them it actually changed ("Changed 3 of 58 choices"), that none changed, or that they already match the backup, plus "Changes paused" or "Changes resumed" when the backup pauses or unpauses them. It used to count every choice in the backup, even ones that were already set that way, and the pause along with them. It only says they match when nothing in the backup was skipped or left off. Skipped choices and Auto reply staying off used to run past the end of that message. Now the whole result stays written under the backup buttons.
- When reading a choices file takes too long, the message says your choices didn't change. It used to warn that a saved file may be cut off, which is only true when saving one.
- If settings can't open from the side menu or the Messenger logo, two short messages now say so and then name the other way in. One message was too long and lost its second half.
- Picking a font file while **Use your own font** is off now says to turn the switch on, instead of telling you to restart Messenger. The file's name stays on the line under the button, where a long one can't push the rest off the message.
- **Use system emoji** loads the phone's emoji font once, even when Messenger asks for it from several threads at the same moment. Each thread could load its own copy before.
- When the message log can't save new messages (the phone's key store is busy, say), it tries again by itself 30 seconds later, then less often while it keeps failing. Those messages used to wait for the next notification and were lost if Messenger closed first.
- **Back to defaults**, the other Quick setups and **Clear log** each show an Undo button at the top of settings for about 10 seconds, and the emptied log shows one too. Clear log still deletes the saved messages and their key right away. Undo writes them back from a copy kept in memory until the button goes, and leaving settings makes the change final. Undo leaves alone any switch you flipped yourself after the change.
- The **Patch controls** and **Restart Messenger** long-press shortcuts now tell your launcher when you use them, so it can rank them by use the way it does Messenger's own chat shortcuts.
- On a Root Mount install, **Patch controls** and **Restart Messenger** never actually showed up on Messenger's icon. Android turned down the icons they used. They show up now.
- Each way back into settings now has its own switch on the App tab, as asked in [HushFacebook#146](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushFacebook/issues/146). Next to **Hide the Menu row** there's **Hide the icon shortcuts**, which takes **Patch controls** and **Restart Messenger** off the menu you get by long-pressing Messenger's home screen icon, and **Ignore the logo long press**. The last way in that's still on can't be switched off: its switch goes gray and says why, and if a saved choice leaves nothing else, the icon shortcuts come back by themselves. Pause and safe mode leave these switches alone, since they're how you get back in to undo either. If the logo long press is what lets the Menu row stay hidden, the row comes back after an update or a repatch until one long press on the logo opens settings again.
- The two icon shortcuts now come from HushMessenger each time Messenger starts, on every install, which is what lets the new switch take them away. After updating, they show up again a moment after you first open Messenger. Messenger's own chat shortcuts can push them down that menu while it's open, so they move back to the top each time you leave Messenger. On a normal install, a copy you pinned to the home screen before this update stops working, so remove it and pin a new one. Copies pinned from now on turn gray while the shortcuts are hidden and work again once they're back.
- When Messenger's chat shortcuts already fill everything Android lets one icon hold, the lowest of them now make room, so **Patch controls** and **Restart Messenger** still get on. Android refused to add them to a full icon before. Those are the same chat shortcuts Android drops when Messenger adds one to a full icon itself.
- In the settings cards, the line between switches now sits below a switch's own buttons (Keep the Profile button, the bubble choices, the font file, message log and auto reply buttons) instead of between the switch and them. The last switch in a card no longer draws a second line just above the card's edge.
- With a keyboard or a remote, the focus outline on a filled settings button (Restart Messenger, Save, the selected tab and filter) is now two rings, a dark and a light one, so it shows up against the button and the page in both themes. The single gray ring was hard to see on the button's own color.
- The settings call each thing by one name now. Controls are the switches, and your choices are what you set them to. The clipboard backup buttons are **Copy choices** and **Paste choices**, since Export and Import sat right next to the file buttons and sounded like them. The third bubble mode is **Android bubbles**, which is what Android calls them. Flipping a control pops up "Hide stories and notes is on" instead of "Hide stories and notes on", and **Fix videos that won't send** says what it does before it names the sound format.
- Two patches got plainer names, in Morphe's patch list and in settings. **Turn off the swipe up for disappearing messages** is now **Ignore the swipe up for disappearing messages**, and **Stop MQTT logging** is now **Stop live connection logging**. Morphe remembers your picks by name, so if you'd left either one out, it'll be picked again. Uncheck it before you repatch.
- With **Hide People tab**, **Hide Notifications tab** or **Hide Meta AI** on, the bar at the bottom now lights up the tab you're actually on. With People hidden, opening Notifications used to light up Menu instead.
- Some accounts get Meta's newer AI agent tab in the bar where the Meta AI tab usually sits. **Hide Meta AI** now hides that one too, and the bar still lights up the right tab. Restart Messenger to see it go.
- On a new install of a patched Messenger, **Bold** and **Italic** no longer show up in the text selection menu while **Bold and italic text** is off. Messenger's very first start turned them on, and they stayed there until you opened Messenger a second time. Updating over an existing install never did this.
- **Hide AI sticker tools** now hides the plain **Generate** button at the start of the sticker keyboard's Suggested row too. Accounts that get more than one sticker maker see that button instead of **Generate AI stickers**, and it opens a menu with AI stickers in it, so the switch used to miss it. The custom and music stickers in that menu are hidden along with it while the switch is on.
- With **Hide AI sticker tools** on, the rest of the Suggested row now moves up to fill the spot where **Generate** or **Generate AI stickers** used to sit. The button went away before, but its slot at the start of the row stayed blank.
- HushMessenger is now built for Morphe's 2.0 patcher, so this release needs **Morphe Manager 2.0.0 or newer**. Update Manager first, then repatch. Every patch still applies to Messenger 584 and the patch count is the same.

### Docs

- The README's troubleshooting list now covers a cloned Messenger showing the settings icon again, chats and calls notifying twice because of the Facebook app, and restoring chats with a PIN when the Google Drive restore errors out.

### Checks

- A release can't go public until the whole app has been walked with that exact bundle on a release phone or an emulator. The publish stage now takes the walk's receipt as `-Walk` and stops before the tag or GitHub if the walk wasn't a full pass, saw a Messenger crash, ran on some other device, or patched with a different bundle.
- `scripts/walk-app.ps1` does that walk. It patches Messenger from the bundle, installs it in place on a leased phone or an emulator, opens both settings pages, a chat and the patched screens, then reads the crash buffer and writes the receipt publish asks for. Any Messenger crash fails the walk. Leaving out a page or a screen makes it partial, and neither one backs a release. The receipt also names the APK patched from the bundle and the one the phone actually ran, and publish wants them to match, so walking whatever was already installed (`-SkipPatch`) only counts when it's that same APK. A crash buffer the walk can't read fails it, and the script exits 0 only for a pass (2 for partial, 1 for a fail). A walk that finds the device busy keeps the APK it patched, so the next try goes straight to the device instead of patching again.
- The development release check, the one a held build goes through, fails when the source has no bundle timestamp. Before, a bundle and a source both missing one counted as a match there. The full release check already refused it, and now it says why.
- A Gradle run through this PC's build wrapper fails when the wrapper itself errors out before Gradle starts. It used to exit 0 and read as a pass.
- Recording a Messenger build with `CompatReport.java --save` refuses an APK that isn't signed by Facebook or Meta, or whose signer apksigner can't read. A record pins the APK's checksum, so a re-signed copy could have been written down as the stock build. The release build stage also checks each stock APK against the checksum in its record before patching it, and CompatReport looks for Python as `py`, `python3` or `python` (never the Windows Store stub) instead of assuming `python`.

## 0.25.0 (2026-10-18)

### Reported issues

- [#64](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/64) New **Open your gallery app** switch under Conversations. With it on, the gallery button in a chat opens your phone's own gallery app (Samsung Gallery, Google Photos or whichever you use) instead of Messenger's photo strip. Pick a photo or a video, one at a time, and it opens in Messenger's editor for that chat, ready to send. Back out of the gallery app and nothing gets sent. If more than one app on your phone can pick photos, Android asks which to use. Choosing Always there lets Samsung Gallery show your videos too. With the switch off, paused, or no gallery app on the phone, the button works the way it always has. It starts off.
- [#58](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/58) Samsung screen recordings that end in "Unable to send" go through with **Fix videos that won't send** on. That's the switch that used to be called Fix shared videos with xHE-AAC sound, so if you choose patches by hand in Morphe, tick it again under its new name. Samsung's recorder adds a tag at the end of the file, and Messenger's own media check rejects the file because of it, stock Messenger too. The switch drops that tag in the copy Messenger sends. The picture and sound stay as they are, and your original file isn't changed.
- [#57](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/57) **Fix videos that won't send** now also works on a video you pick inside a chat, not only one you share into Messenger from another app. Its xHE-AAC sound is converted to regular AAC in a copy in Messenger's cache, so the video sends instead of failing with "Unable to send". Your original file isn't changed, and old copies get cleaned up as new ones are made.
- [#28](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/28) **Slide chats in and out** measures Messenger's short freezes against your screen's own refresh rate now, instead of assuming 60 Hz. On a 90 or 120 Hz screen a freeze of a few frames gets skipped over rather than counted as part of the slide. Page and business chats slide now too. Messenger opens them in an older chat screen, and the slide used to skip that one.
- [#59](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/59) New **Stop app list and device ID sharing** switch under Privacy. Messenger goes through your installed apps looking for other Meta apps like Facebook and Instagram, then asks them for the device ID Meta uses to link its apps on one phone. With this on it gets an empty app list and skips the asking, so it keeps the ID it already has. It's experimental and starts off.
- [#62](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/62) New **Mark as read button** switch under Privacy, for people who use **Hide read receipts**. It puts Messenger's own Mark as read icon in a chat's top bar. Opening the chat still sends nothing. When you want the other person to see Seen, tap the icon and the chat turns read on your phone too. If the bar is already full, the icon takes the chat details button's spot, since tapping the chat's name opens that page anyway, so the call buttons stay where they are. It's experimental and starts off.
- [#51](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/51) **Hide Facebook shortcuts** has a **Keep the Profile button** switch right under it. Turn it on and the Profile button stays on a chat's details page, so you can still open the other person's Facebook profile, while the rest of the Facebook shortcuts stay hidden. It starts off, so nothing changes unless you turn it on.
- [#68](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/68) The README's install help covers `signatures do not match` after you've already uninstalled Messenger. It usually means another copy is still installed in Secure Folder, a work profile or a second user, and the note says where to look and how Clone install gets around it.
- [#74](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/74) With **Use the phone's camera app** on, tapping **Edit** on a photo or video you picked in a chat opens Messenger's editor again. The phone's camera only takes over when you tap the chat's camera button. Edit goes through the same camera screen inside Messenger, so it used to open your camera app and then drop you back in the chat.
- [#66](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/66), [#72](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/72) Threads links and Instagram video links open again when the Threads or Instagram on your phone is one you patched yourself. Messenger hands those links to a launcher that only starts apps signed by Meta, and when it says no, nothing happens at all. **Restore screens on re-signed builds** now opens the link in that app the normal way when that happens, or in your browser if the app can't take it.
- [#73](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/73), [#77](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/77) Every arm64 Messenger 583.0.0.29.95 on APKMirror patches now, 13 builds in all. That includes the nodpi ones Morphe Manager's download button opens (346611418 and 346611435), which used to stop with "version code 346611418 is not supported". Meta publishes one build per screen density under the same version name. The new ones turned out to match the two layouts the patches already handle, so nothing about what the patches do changes. The README lists every version code with its SHA-256. When a later build isn't supported yet, the error now opens with "This Messenger build (version code N) isn't supported yet". Manager shows the first patch that stopped, usually **Allow chat bubbles**, so these reports looked like a bubbles bug.
- [#76](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/76) **Send photos at original quality** keeps an HD photo's full size on Messenger 583. That version shrinks photos in its own code on the phone, so even with HD picked a 48 MP photo went out at 4096 pixels on its long side. With the switch on and HD picked, a JPEG up to 20 MB now goes out at the size it was taken, still without its location and camera details. Photos sent without HD stay at Messenger's usual size, and when the phone is short on memory a photo is made only as small as it has to be.

### New

- New **Hold stories at the end** switch under Navigation. When a story finishes, it stays on screen instead of jumping to the next one, so you move on with a tap or a swipe. It works the same for photos and videos, and after the last story the viewer stays open until you close it. It's experimental and starts off.

### Changed and fixed

- Morphe Manager lists every change of the release your Messenger was patched with, not only a short summary. For that one release Manager shows the patch source's description instead of the changelog, and the description was a single paragraph while older releases listed their changes. It now carries the release's changelog groups after the summary. You'll see it starting with this release.
- On a phone set to Arabic, Hebrew, Persian or another right-to-left language, HushMessenger settings read left to right now, like the English they're written in. The page used to be mirrored, and periods and numbers landed on the wrong side of their sentences.
- Searching in HushMessenger settings moves the search box up to the top of the page while the keyboard is open, so the matching switches show right under it. Before, the box stayed where it was and every match sat behind the keyboard until you closed it.
- The README's help for a source that shows an old version pointed at v0.23.0. It points at the latest release now, so it can't fall behind again.
- **Fix videos that won't send** ties the converted copy of a video you pick in a chat to that exact video. Two videos with the same size and date could end up sharing one copy, and a video edited under the same name could send the version from before the edit. Each conversion also works in files of its own now, so two sends at once can't get in each other's way.
- When **Fix videos that won't send** can't convert a shared video's sound, the copy Messenger sends is left exactly as it was. A Samsung recording used to lose its tag before the conversion started, so a conversion that failed partway still changed the file.
- A video with more than one xHE-AAC sound track, like a film with two languages, is now left for Messenger to handle as it is. **Fix videos that won't send** keeps only one sound track when it converts, so it used to drop the others.
- **Fix videos that won't send** has limits now. A video over 1 GB, a phone with less than 256 MB free, a conversion that runs past 10 minutes or a send you cancel goes out as it is, without a conversion. Two videos convert at a time at most, and the converted copies stay within 3 GB of Messenger's cache, clearing copies no send has used in the last hour when it needs room. Two conversions running together count the space the other one needs and keep checking what's free while they work, so between them they still stop at 256 MB.
- **Fix videos that won't send** checks that the converted copy is as long as the video before sending it. A conversion that stopped partway, or a picked video that only came through in part, used to go out shorter than the original.
- Messenger closing partway through a shared video's conversion no longer leaves the half-made files behind in its storage. The next shared video clears them once they're old enough that no conversion could still be using them, and a send you cancel while a file is being read isn't counted as a failure anymore.

## 0.24.0 (2026-10-14)

This release moves HushMessenger to Messenger 583.0.0.29.95, all six arm64 builds of it. 582 isn't accepted anymore, so update Messenger before you patch. It also adds new switches, among them Pin more chats, Bold and italic text, Hide People tab and Stop nearby scans, and settings get a restart button, Quick setups and a Report a problem row.

### Reported issues

- [#54](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/54) HushMessenger settings no longer leave a card of their own in Recents next to Messenger's. You'll still see them there while they're open on screen. Once you go Home or switch apps, the card drops out.
- [#55](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/55) Replying with Hide read receipts on now allows Seen only for that chat in the same account. A reply no longer opens a brief Seen window in other chats.
- [#47](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/47), [#56](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/56) New **Pin more chats** switch under Inbox. Messenger stops you at three pinned chats, and this raises the limit to 20. It's experimental, since Messenger's servers may still hold you to three, and like every switch it starts off.
- [#52](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/52) New **Stop nearby scans** switch under Privacy. It stops Messenger from reading your phone's list of nearby Wi-Fi networks, and it stops the timer Messenger can run while it's open to check your location and scan for Wi-Fi networks and Bluetooth devices. Messenger also stops seeing the name and hardware address of the Wi-Fi network you're on. It gets the same blank answers Android gives an app that can't see your location, so your connection, calls and signal readings work as before. It's experimental, since Messenger hasn't started those scans on the test phones yet. It starts off, and the Quick setups don't turn it on.
- [#51](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/51) **Hide Facebook shortcuts** hides the Menu tab's Subscriptions section too, the one with Facebook Plus, Meta Verified and Meta One. Messenger had moved those out of Also from Meta into a section of their own, so the switch stopped catching them.
- [#49](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/49) With **Spoof package version** in, Messenger's own checks of its version code still see the build it really is. Before, Messenger's component manager noticed the raised number didn't match the APK and flagged it in its log, and crash data carried the raised number. Only Messenger's reads of itself change. Google Play services, Facebook and every other app's version read as Android reports them.
- [#48](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/48) A font file you pick under **Use your own font** can now be made smaller or larger, from 70% to 160%, and its text can be moved up or down in steps of 2% of its size. That's for a font that looks small or sits too high in a message bubble. Only HushMessenger's copy of the file changes, so your phone's own font and text size settings stay as they are. Restart Messenger to see a change.
- [#44](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/44) **Stop MQTT logging** is a new Privacy switch that starts off. It drops the health report and push logging Messenger's MQTT client sends, and when that client uses the health report as its keepalive, it sends Messenger's plain ping instead. Messenger sends most of its traffic another way, so there's often little for it to drop. Messages, calls and active status keep working.
- [#43](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/43) **Hide People tab** and **Hide Notifications tab** are new Navigation switches. Each one takes its tab out of the bar at the bottom once Messenger restarts, and both start off.
- [#42](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/42) **Bold and italic text** is a new Conversations switch that starts off. With it on, select words you typed and pick **Bold** or **Italic** in the text menu (it can be behind the three dots). Picking the same one again turns the words back. They come out as special Unicode letters, which show on any phone. Keycap emoji like 1️⃣ stay as they are, and picking an entry on text you can't edit shows a short tip. It works in the chat box too. On a Samsung phone you may need to turn on **Messenger Bold** and **Messenger Italic** once under **Manage apps** in that menu.
- [#34](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushMessenger/issues/34) With **Use system emoji** on, a Like in the chat list shows your phone's thumbs up instead of an empty box whenever Messenger's emoji font can't draw it. Messenger's Like is a character only its own font can draw, so a new install showed a box there even without HushMessenger, until Messenger downloaded its font and restarted. Android 9 can't put Messenger's font behind the phone's, so there the thumbs up always stands in.

### New

- Long-press the Messenger logo at the top of Chats, or the Menu tab's title, to open HushMessenger settings. It's a way in from inside Messenger even when your launcher has no long-press menu, and it works while changes are paused. It comes with **Open settings from menu**, and the App tab's tip about getting back in now mentions it. Messenger's own long press on the title still runs first.
- New **Hide Mark as read** switch. It takes Mark as read out of the menu you get when you press and hold a chat in your chat list, so you can't mark a chat read by accident. Mark as unread stays where it is. Like every switch it starts off.
- **Bold and italic text** covers the letters A to Z (accents are fine) and digits, and Italic covers just those letters. Picking one on Russian or Arabic words, or Italic on numbers, shows a short tip saying why nothing changed. Mixed text changes the letters it can and leaves the rest alone. The switch's description and the README name the limits too.

### Changed and fixed

- HushMessenger now patches Messenger 583.0.0.29.95 and no longer accepts 582.0.0.61.92. It takes six arm64 builds of 583, the five APKMirror lists plus build 346611440. Meta renumbered several server flags and moved a few checks around in 583, so every switch's hooks were found again on each build, and Morphe Desktop patched and rebuilt all six.
- With **Hide Meta AI** on, typing @ in a chat no longer suggests Meta AI or its imagine entries. Messenger had started building that list a newer way that went around the switch. Silent and the rest of the list stay.
- A full message log opens without freezing. The log builds 20 messages at a time and adds the next ones as you scroll toward the end, so every saved message is still there, newest first. Before, a log near its 500-message limit held the screen still for about two seconds while it opened.
