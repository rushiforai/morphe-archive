First release.

- **Android Auto audio sync** (universal patch): holds video back so it lines up with the sound from the car over wireless Android Auto. Tested to apply to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and Reddit.
- **SoundSync app**: set the delay and when to apply it, with a quick-settings button to adjust it over a playing video.

In Morphe Manager, add this repository as a patch source, then tick "Android Auto audio sync" under Universal patches (Expert mode).
