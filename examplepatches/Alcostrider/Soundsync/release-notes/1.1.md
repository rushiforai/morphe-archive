Fixes "Only while connected to Android Auto".

- **Android Auto audio sync**: now asks Android Auto itself whether it's connected (the same check apps like Spotify use). The old check relied on the phone's car mode, which wireless Android Auto doesn't turn on, so the delay never kicked in. Repatch your apps to get the fix.
- **SoundSync app**: the delay is now labelled "Video held back by:" (750 here is the same as −750 in Fermata), and "When to apply" shows whether Android Auto is connected right now.
