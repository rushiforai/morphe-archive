# Remove Log out

"Log out" is gone from the menu's accounts, so you can't log out of every account at once by mistake.

- BaconReader's "Log out" logs out of every account at once and keeps them listed, each to be logged in to again. It
  sits right above the accounts.
- Each account's ✕ still removes that account, and "Add account" stays.

No settings.
