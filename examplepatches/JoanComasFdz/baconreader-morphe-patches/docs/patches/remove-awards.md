# Remove awards

Award icons and award actions, which no longer work, are gone.

- Posts and comments show no award icons, in the post list and on the post screen. Reddit no longer reports awards.
- "Give Award" and "View Award Details" are gone from the post menu and from the action bars of posts and comments.
  Giving awards used Reddit's coins, which are discontinued.
- The two "Hide Reddit Awards" settings, in List View and Detail View, are gone: they no longer changed anything.
- Screen readers name the action bars' buttons after their actions ("Upvote", "Save", "More options"…).

No settings.
