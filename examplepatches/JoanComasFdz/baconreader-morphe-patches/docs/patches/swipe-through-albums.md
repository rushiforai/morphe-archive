# Swipe through albums

Swipe left or right on a Reddit gallery or an Imgur album to go to its next or previous image.

- Swipe left anywhere on the image for the next one, and right for the previous one, as the arrows at the screen's
  sides do. The arrows and the thumbnail strip keep working.
- At the first image a swipe right does nothing, and at the last a swipe left.
- Zoomed in, a swipe moves around the image, as before. A pinch never changes image.
- In an Imgur album, a swipe on a video or a GIF goes to the next or previous item too, and a tap still pauses and plays
  it.

No settings.
