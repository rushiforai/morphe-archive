# Select text

"Select text" in the menus of posts and comments lets you select and copy any part of their text.

- "Select text" shows right after "Copy" in a post's menu, and in "More options" of a post's or a comment's action
  bar. BaconReader's "Copy" only copies the whole text.
- It opens a dialog with the post's title and text, or the comment's text, as BaconReader shows them. Long press to
  select any part and copy it; "CLOSE" closes the dialog.
- Spoilers show in plain sight, and links open with a tap.
- A table drawn by Show tables shows as text, one line a row, its cells separated by " | ".

No settings.
