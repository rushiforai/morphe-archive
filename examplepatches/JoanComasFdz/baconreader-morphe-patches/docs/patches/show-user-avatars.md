# Show user avatars

The author's avatar shows before the name, as on Reddit's website.

- With the setting on, avatars show in comments, on the reply screen ("Reply to … in r/… as …") and in the inbox.
- The left menu always shows each account's avatar before its name: the account in use, and the accounts in its list.

**Settings** (BaconReader → Settings → Patches, under "Comments"):
- **Show user avatars** (off by default): shows the author's avatar before the name in comments, on the reply screen
  and in the inbox.
- **Avatar size** (Normal by default): "Normal", as tall as the text, "Medium", "Large", as on Reddit's website, or
  "Extra large".
- **Avatar background** (on by default): a faint disc behind avatars, as on Reddit's website.

Avatar size and Avatar background also apply to the avatars in the left menu.
