# Implementation Changelog (`v3.16.0`)

1. **`RexDialogs.java`**: Fixed lines 213–214 (`viewerUnconnected == null`), redesigned `showFriendshipStatusSheet` (`23113.png`) and `showStoryMentionsSheet` (`23125.png`).
2. **`BaseSharedPref.java` & `FlagsSharedPref.java`**: Fixed `getString(StringSetting)` to read from `this` (`Constants.REC_FLAGS` for `FlagsSharedPref`) rather than `SharedPref`, and added synchronous `flushPreferences()` on `FlagsSharedPref.setStringPref` and `clearAll()`.
3. **`RecommendedFlagPreference.java` & `ScreenBuilder.java` & `ButtonPref.java`**: Rewrote `RecommendedFlagPreference` using `InstagramPreferenceStyle.createPreferenceView` (14dp card margins, 22dp corner radius, `#303741` border, semantic Tabler SVG icons, inline `Default`/`Enabled`/`Disabled` pill badge, inline `Reset` pill, and `ListView` recycling-safe `onBindView` listener binding) and grouped `Recommended flags` into 3 categories (`23212.png`–`23214.png`).
4. **`ReelDownloadButtonPatch.kt` & `ReelDownload.java`**: Mounted Reels Download (`index 0`) and Rex Options (`index 1`) at the top of the Reels right UFI column above the Heart icon, using `OpticalReelIconDrawable` (`0.31f` radius, `1.9dp` stroke) (`23115.png`, `23127.png`).
5. **`DownloadUtils.java`**: Fixed Feed Hexagon left-cluster insertion (`countVisibleFeedCounters <= 2` check on outer `leftRow`) (`23120.png`) and moved Story custom controls to a Top-Right Story Action Cluster (`@N` mentions pill, `⤓` Download, `⚙` Settings) while hooking `toolbar_like_container` for Story Likes (`23122.png`, `23126.png`).
6. **`Entity.java`, `MediaData.java`, `UserData.java`, `UserFriendshipStatus.java`**: Prevented `Entity` double-wrapping (`23125.png`) and added reflective fallbacks for mentions and friendship status fields.
7. **`StoryLikeHistoryRecorder.java` & `StoryLikeHistoryActivity.java`**: Added universal story-heart tap recording and 24h Activity Bar Chart (`23126.png`).
8. **`ChangeLikeAnimationPatch.java`, `ChangeLikeAnimationPatch.kt`, `LikeAnimationPreference.java`**: Enabled dynamic runtime switching across all 4 Like Animation presets with an animated rings-heart preview dialog (`23128.png`).
9. **`CustomFont.java`, `FontStorage.java`, `RexRuntimeFeatures.java`, `HookFlags.java`, `RestorePrefFragment.java`**: Enabled live UI/Story fonts (`14` bundled iOS fonts), Emoji packs (`Twemoji`, `OpenMoji`, `Blobmoji`, `iOS 17.4`), `mc_overrides.json` runtime loading into `HookFlags`, and universal `.json`/`.xml`/`.zip` backup import.
