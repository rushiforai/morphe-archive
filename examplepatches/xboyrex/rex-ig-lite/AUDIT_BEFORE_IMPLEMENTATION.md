## 1. Root-Cause Findings & Remediations

| Audit ID | Severity | Component / File | Root Cause | Implemented Fix | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`AUD-STORY-001`** | High | `StoryButton.java` | `VIEW_STORY_MENTIONS`, `ENABLE_DOWNLOAD`, and `DEBUG` were cached in `static final` fields at class load time, ignoring runtime toggle changes until app restart. | Replaced static cached fields with dynamic runtime helper checks (`isViewStoryMentionsEnabled()`, `isDownloadEnabled()`, `isDebugEnabled()`) and wired `Story Quick Settings` & `InstaRex Story Options`. | **FIXED** |
| **`AUD-STORY-002`** | High | `DownloadUtils.java` (`addStoryDownloadButton`) | Story bottom toolbar (`toolbar_buttons_container`) only injected a single Download button and lacked direct **Hidden Mentions (`@`)** and **Story Settings / Options** buttons shown in Screenshots `#25` (`122305`) and `#26` (`122336`). | Upgraded `addStoryDownloadButton` to inject 3 Tabler icon buttons (`STORY_DOWNLOAD_BUTTON_TAG`, `STORY_MENTIONS_BUTTON_TAG` with `at-sign`, and `STORY_OPTIONS_BUTTON_TAG` with `settings`) + wired `RexDialogs.showStoryOptionsSheet` & `RexDialogs.showSurfaceQuickSettingsDialog(context, "STORY")`. | **FIXED** |
| **`AUD-REEL-001`** | High | `ReelDownload.java` & `ReelDownloadButtonPatch.kt` | Reels right-side Litho UFI (`ClipsSaveButtonComponent`) only cloned the Save component once for Direct Download, missing a dedicated **Reels Settings / Options** button in the vertical action column (Screenshots `#21` `122057` & `#27` `122433`). | Added `ReelDownload.isOptionsEnabled()` and `ReelDownload.registerOptions(component)` in `ReelDownload.java` and injected a second registered `ClipsSaveButtonComponent` copy in `ReelDownloadButtonPatch.kt` using existing registers (`2, 15`), opening the Reels Options Sheet on tap and Reels Quick Settings on long-press. | **FIXED** |
| **`AUD-FEED-001`** | High | `DownloadUtils.java` (`addFeedDownloadButton`) | Disabling `FEED_DOWNLOAD_BUTTON` removed both the Download button and the Hexagon Options button, preventing independent icon visibility control (`Disable 'Download' Icon` vs `Disable 'InstaRex Options' Icon` in Screenshot `#13` `121921`). | Decoupled `isFeedDownloadButtonEnabled()` and `isFeedOptionsButtonEnabled()` with `Settings.DISABLE_FEED_DOWNLOAD_ICON` and `Settings.DISABLE_FEED_OPTIONS_ICON`, and wired long-press on the Feed Hexagon button to `RexDialogs.showSurfaceQuickSettingsDialog(context, "FEED")`. | **FIXED** |
| **`AUD-SEARCH-001`** | Medium | `ActionBarPatch.java` & `RexRuntimeFeatures.java` | Screenshot `#1` (`121210`) and `#16` (`121952`) specify `Long-Press Search to Open Profile` (copy `@username` or profile URL -> hold `search_tab` -> jump straight to profile), which was not wired on the bottom navigation bar. | Added `Settings.LONG_PRESS_SEARCH_OPEN_PROFILE`, `ActionBarPatch.bindSearchTabIfPresent(activity)`, and `RexRuntimeFeatures.openProfileFromClipboardOrPrompt(context)` with automatic clipboard `@username`/URL extraction and interactive fallback dialog. | **FIXED** |
| **`AUD-THEME-001`** | Medium | `InstagramPreferenceStyle.java`, `RexDialogs.java`, `RexAboutView.java` | Single static color palette (`#15181D` / `#23C6D8`) without user-selectable Color Modes. | Implemented `InstagramPreferenceStyle.ColorPalette` with 6 curated AMOLED & Glass Color Modes (`Rex Cyan AMOLED`, `Prime Crimson Rose`, `Royal Violet Glass`, `Emerald Matrix Pro`, `Sunset Amber Gold`, `Monochrome Titanium`) selectable from **UI Tweaks** and **Quick Access**. | **FIXED** |
| **`AUD-CONFIG-001`** | High | `SABRAHCADAVER_V6.json` -> `INSTAREX_PRO_CONFIG_V6.json` | Uploaded `SABRAHCADAVER_V6.json` (`3,796` MobileConfig keys) contained foreign strings/watermarks in keys `75465:`, `82030:`, `78295:`, `70497:`, `93476:`, `39443:`, and `79665:`. | Sanitized all 7 keys, rebranded to `INSTAREX_PRO_CONFIG_V6.json`, bundled in `assets/configs/INSTAREX_PRO_CONFIG_V6.json` and `res/raw/instarex_pro_config_v6.json`, and wired 1-tap `Apply InstaRex Pro Config V6` and `Export InstaRex Pro Config V6` in Developer Options, Quick Access, and Backup & Restore. | **FIXED** |
