# De Tijd

Target: original Play APKS **5.6.1 / 5060100**, Android 8.0+. The original six controls are available in dev prerelease [v1.21.0-dev.1](https://github.com/johnneerdael/dutch-applications-patches/releases/tag/v1.21.0-dev.1). The follow-up is available in [v1.22.0-dev.1](https://github.com/johnneerdael/dutch-applications-patches/releases/tag/v1.22.0-dev.1): it adds direct anonymous De essentie display, automatic in-app promotion suppression and a separate default-off Mijn Tijd option. [Technical audit](../../detijd-audit.md) · [Research](../../research/2026-10-30/detijd.md) · [Tracking destinations](../../TRACKING_DESTINATIONS.md).

The anonymous app received 21 and 27 paragraphs for two sampled articles while its separate paywall requests returned HTTP 403 with `hasAccess:false`. Its UI still showed the subscriber wall and reduced display. Read full articles changes that presentation decision, preserving normal article requests, the anonymous client token and real subscription state. It includes Hide ads because the full-display branch also inserts advertisements. This evidence does not establish every article or other server-controlled benefits.

| Current candidate control | Default | Effect |

| Hide Mijn Tijd (expert) | Off | Hide only its bottom-bar button. Keep De essentie, routes, indices, weekend labels and manual account access. |

Mijn Tijd works with a **free De Tijd account; no paid membership is required**, as confirmed by the user. Keep its button by default. Recommend the expert option only when the user does not intend to use an account. That option does not change login, subscription or content state.

The normal anonymous Moments request, `GET content/feed/timeboxed/urn:agencygroup:tijd/MOMENTS`, returned three articles with six content paragraphs each. The follow-up changes only function 23982's two presentation branches, retaining the real account state, ordinary query, hooks and empty/weekend paths. The standard-six emulator candidate displayed De essentie directly without an account: `7u`, `12u` and `17u` sections and three long text views of 169, 325 and 268 characters. This is sampled content availability, not a guarantee for every future feed.

All configured destinations above are artifact observations, not proof of emitted traffic in every state. The bounded captures concern anonymous publisher article/feed delivery and local SDK message fixtures; they are not a comprehensive before/after tracking capture. Automatic in-app-message suppression applies regardless of campaign content, so it can also suppress a non-marketing message delivered through that executor.

All seven individual selections, all seven together and the six standard controls pass real Morphe FULL patching, expected applied-selection reports, signing and independent decoded-output checks. The current verifier checks **23 Hermes contracts**, complete original reconstruction and the footer, **516 native original/retained contracts**, **504 navigation cases** and **16 Moments gate cases**, plus functional manifest state. The actual Kotlin transformer passes **127 non-empty control subsets** under direct/forward/reverse/repeated composition and ten corruption/policy rejection checks. A separate review retains 32 protected JS functions and 24 native promotion/push/manual-access contracts. The tested local bundle builds with `python3 scripts/build_detijd.py`; the follow-up is published in [v1.22.0-dev.1](https://github.com/johnneerdael/dutch-applications-patches/releases/tag/v1.22.0-dev.1).

The original local text-only `ScheduleAction` fixture returned SDK `READY` and opened the real fullscreen activity. The patched typed fixture returned `SKIP` through the executor, dispatcher and engine; its actual consumer completed with `true` and zero adapter displays. A separate real persisted schedule moved from prepared to idle, ran schedule and asset cleanup and never entered the display executor. Retained console proof is `apks/research/detijd-followup/popup-patched-console.log`, with `popup-runtime-summary.json`; typed in-memory completion and persisted-queue acceptance are separate checks.

With the expert option enabled, the emulator displayed exactly Nieuws, Recent, De essentie and Meer, with no Mijn Tijd button; all four retained routes were visited successfully. The default-six candidate kept all five normal buttons, including Mijn Tijd. Three new portable navigation/Moments unit tests cover the semantic helpers. See the audit for exact artifact identities and bounded runtime evidence.

The earlier six-control combined candidate opened the homepage on a fresh API 34 arm64 emulator without automatic consent/onboarding/service-banner UI or a signed-in account. Reopening the first sampled article kept its real HTTP 403/`hasAccess:false` paywall response while exposing multiple delivered text paragraphs: 11 unique long text views, 3,341 visible characters across seven scrolled layouts, no subscriber wall and no AndroidRuntime or ReactNativeJS error observed through that check. That historical release used 21 Hermes and 496 native contracts; its hashes remain in the audit.

The USB phone's genuine free-account session was preserved during the follow-up: no reinstall, data wipe or logout. Its existing installation signing certificate prevents an in-place desktop-signed update. The free-account Mijn Tijd claim comes from user confirmation and source inspection, not a paid or logged-in emulator test. The candidate was not installed over that phone session. Live push delivery, the exact original remote promotion, other Android versions, media, editions, text-to-speech, gifted access, offline reading and future remote code remain outside this acceptance evidence.

## Stable approval

On 30 October 2026, the owner confirmed that De Tijd works correctly on the phone and explicitly approved release on main, requesting no further testing. Promote the exact De Tijd implementation from v1.22.0-dev.2: all seven controls, with the six standard controls enabled and Hide Mijn Tijd (expert) disabled by default. Mijn Tijd remains available with a free account; no paid subscription is required. De essentie displays the feed supplied by the normal API without an account or the marketing/“Ga verder” page. No additional local or device tests were performed after approval. Earlier validation and runtime evidence, and its limits for exhaustive traffic, push delivery, future feeds and other devices, remain unchanged.
