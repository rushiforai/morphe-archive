## Intent and approach

Provide two default-on controls: **Hide ads** and **Disable tracking**. Tracking
includes Hide ads, because loaded advertising SDKs and creatives have their own
measurement paths. Preserve journeys, maps and precise functional location,
saved data and API identity, tickets/wallet/payment outcomes, rental/lock
operations, notifications and genuine consent choices.

Three approaches were considered. Global network blocking would affect shared
travel, Firebase configuration and payment services. Stubbing individual ad
loaders would require artificial callbacks and could strand reserved views.
Use the existing key-specific no-ad branches, product-specific measurement
controls and a typed persisted-event filter instead. This implementation choice
uses the user's existing authorization to build the researched apps and publish
dev candidates; phone approval remains the main-publication gate.

No paid ad-removal tier was established. Transport purchases and Play licensing
are separate from advertising. Do not invent subscriptions, receipts, ticket
success, consent grants, successful measurement sends or a new server identity.
