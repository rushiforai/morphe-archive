## Signing, integrity and limits

**Seen:** independent original signer hashes above; re-signing changes certificate identity and ordinary Android update compatibility. Package/session-provider signature permissions, FCM installation identity, external-offer/Billing behavior and PSPDF/native licensing can have certificate/package/server dependencies. Existing original's two signer details must be preserved in evidence rather than assuming the patch signer can update it. Article anonymous fallback probes did not use the app signature and therefore prove nothing about authenticated signed-client checks.

No app-owned `CertificatePinner.Builder.add`, explicit OkHttp certificate-pinner installation, Play Integrity token request or package-signature self-check call was found by the bounded full-decode searches. **Unknown, not absent:** dynamic SDK/native pinning, WebView certificate checks, remote auth restrictions and paid/API-key signer restrictions need a re-signed runtime test. Chromium's media-integrity boundary/PSPDF document-integrity class names are capabilities, not evidence of app Play Integrity enforcement. Neither should be removed wholesale.
