### P2 · Opening Hidden Flags creates overrides without a toggle

The settings screen calls `ensureDefault` for every row. It persists `false` on first view. Startup treats any stored key as an explicit override and reapplies that value. After opening the screen and cold-starting the keyboard, every previously untouched catalog flag can therefore be forced Off. Both review APKs contain the `ensureDefault` call in `buildScreen`.

Preserve three states in storage: absent, explicitly On and explicitly Off. Construct the settings screen without writing defaults. Account for entries created by earlier builds. Legacy `true` records can carry forward. A legacy `false` record alone cannot distinguish an explicit choice from a screen-generated default, so define its migration behavior before testing an in-place upgrade.

Acceptance on clean app data should include opening the screen and restarting without toggling, then confirming no overrides were created. Separately toggle On and Off, restart after each, and check the effective flag and consumer. Include an upgrade case with old, screen-generated preferences. Use a fresh commit-derived bundle for the repaired implementation.
