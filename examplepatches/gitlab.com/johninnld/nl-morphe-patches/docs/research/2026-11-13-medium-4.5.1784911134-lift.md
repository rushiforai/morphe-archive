## Why the native reader stays on 4.5.1784911033

The reader republishes locked articles through the app's own paragraph components and needs
five bindings that this build renamed or restructured beyond a mechanical lift:

- The requested-post coroutine moved (`xub` → `pmb`, data class `gvb` → `ymb`) and the
  flow-combine seam is gone: the old `aoj.x(Flow,Flow,Continuation)` call is now a
  `cb5.b`/`f80`/`uq2` operator chain, so the wrap point must be re-derived at instruction
  level instead of at a named join.
- The repository became `fab` and its refresh-event field type changed with it.
- The Coil keyer (`vw.a(r67,ika)`) and its request class (`r67`) both moved to unrelated
  classes, and the article fragments `PostFragment` / `PostPageFragment2` no longer exist —
  `com/medium/android/donkey/post/` is gone and the surviving fragment lives at
  `com/medium/android/postpage/ui/PostPageFragment`.
- The reader extension compiles against 70 hand-written compile-only stubs of the app's
  obfuscated ABI. Every stub name still exists in this build but now belongs to an unrelated
  class (`pa2` is a Dialog, `qye` an enum), so the stubs and the six extension sources would
  need a full coordinated regeneration.

Pinning that patch to its audited build keeps it working for anyone who supplies the older
archive without shipping a hook that would throw at runtime on this one.
