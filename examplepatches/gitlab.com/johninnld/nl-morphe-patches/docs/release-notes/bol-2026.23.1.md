# bol 2026.23.1 changes

Based on [Wouter075's bol patches](https://github.com/wouter075/bol-patches/tree/350a044f83d0e3bf73816ed8be04aa10136f9b0d), originally written for 2026.22.1. Credit to **Wouter075** for the original sponsored-content, tracking and Play-license patches. This integration extends and fixes their coverage; it is more than a compatibility port.

- Add support for **2026.23.1 build 430517**, with the actual Android 12L minimum and complete split input.
- Fix Home display advertisements that arrive through `HomePageTop`, a separate response from the original `DisplayAdsAndroid` target. Clear advertising data in cached/copy model construction as well.
- Suppress the separate sponsored flag in product-list requests. Filter marked sponsored products out of search and shopping-assistant product lists, commentary and comparisons, instead of removing their disclosure labels. Remove sponsored brand cards and the additional product-page sponsored recommendation/ad fields.
- Add **Hide promotional content** for top/flex banners, brand campaigns, campaign banner groups, game/day-deal promotions, shopping-concept promotions, campaign suggestions and promotional feeds. Remove the separately loaded Trending Deals/Home merchandising slots and seasonal Sint/Christmas campaign backgrounds found during emulator acceptance. Remove the independent View all offers Home footer, which remains even when the content slots are empty. Disable Airship in-app automation while retaining push and the message center. Product prices and checkout discounts are preserved.
- Add **Hide consent screens** for data-usage and data-sharing prompts, including renewed requests. Also skip the offer-marketing notification opt-in steps exposed by a fresh-data run, without granting permissions or changing manual notification settings. Map local consent choices to declined and require Disable tracking. This does not submit acceptance or rewrite server-side account consent.
- Extend **Disable tracking** to stop RudderStack initialization, config downloads and persisted queue replay; stop first-party `StartGA4Session` and experiment-measurement mutations; suppress AppsFlyer identifiers/PII entry points; disable Sentry automatic startup; and mask Airship analytics, tag/attribute collection and on-device AI profiling even when enabled in saved settings.

Validation status and the complete old-versus-new findings inventory are recorded in [the analysis report](../privacy/bol-2026.23.1.md). Runtime verification is required before describing these changes as accepted or publishing them.
