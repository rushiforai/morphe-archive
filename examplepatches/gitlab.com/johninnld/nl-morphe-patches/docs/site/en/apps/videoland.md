## Patches

| Patch | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Disable tracking (Android - v119.2)** | On | Stops the supported optional usage statistics, advertising identifiers and diagnostics. |
| **Hide ads (Android - v119.2)** | On | Removes the supported ads before or around videos. Ads already included in the programme or stream may remain. |
| **Skip consent screens (Android - v119.2)** | On | Rejects optional cookies and skips automatic notification setup. Notifications start off; manual choices remain available. |
| **Remember last profile (experimental) (Android - v119.2)** | Off | Selects the last profile used by this account on an online cold launch. You can still switch profiles manually. |

## Advertising and tracking services

The ad and tracking controls cover the supported optional activity from: Tealium, Snowplow, NPAW, New Relic, Google / Firebase, Meta, Salesforce analytics, Usabilla, JOIN measurement.

Blocking measurement does not block every function from the same provider. Essential app services can still connect.

## Before using

In Manager, select the patches labelled **Android - v119.2** for build **1637**. To enable profile recall, select the experimental patch and choose a profile once.

Use the correct phone or Android TV build. Accounts and content access still follow the service’s rules. These patches do not grant a subscription; broadcast, stream-inserted or Cast-receiver ads may remain.
