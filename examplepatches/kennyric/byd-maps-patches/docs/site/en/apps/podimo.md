## Patches

| Patch | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Android Auto show grid browse** | On | Rebuilds the Android Auto browse around podcast series: For-you grids of series covers, every episode of a show on drill-in, no empty Library tab for guests, and any car client may connect. |
| **Guest session playback host** | On | Lets guests play supported episodes without signing in. Use with Unlock Premium playback. |
| **Unlock Premium playback** | On | Replaces Premium buttons with play buttons, enables playing supported episodes and hides trial offers. |

## Before using

Use all three patches together for Android Auto; the playback patches also work on the phone alone. They do not create a subscription. The service can still refuse playback; downloads, audiobooks and availability across regions or accounts are not guaranteed. No tracking-blocking patch is included.
