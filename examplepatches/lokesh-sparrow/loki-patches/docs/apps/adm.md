## Features

| Feature | What you get |
|---|---|
| 🚫 **Remove ads (ADM)** | Removes ADM's ads, including the Google and AppBrain banners, the red "Disable Ads" menu item, and the Disable Ads and Telegram bars under the download list |

## How-to

### Open the Loki Patches menu

- **Toolbar overflow menu** (the three dots at the top right) → **Loki Patches**.
- Long press the ADM icon on your home screen → **Loki Patches**.

### Notes

- ADM's only paid entitlement is "Disable Ads"; removing the ads is the whole purchase. Buy ADM Pro if you rely on it.
- The app's own "Disable Ads" bar and Telegram group bar are part of ADM and are not removed.
- Use the APKM from APKMirror.
