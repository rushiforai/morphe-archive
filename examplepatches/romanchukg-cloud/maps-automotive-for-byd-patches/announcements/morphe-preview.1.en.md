🗺 Maps Automotive for BYD — Morphe preview 1 (TEST)

The Automotive edition for Android head units, independent of phone Maps. Sign-in uses MicroG-RE 7.2.1.

Add our source through Morphe → Sources → + → Remote. Morphe downloads our patches and lists Automotive 26.09; supply your own matching original APK.

Optional BYD reader and CCS2 BEV routing need ADB authorization and BYD background/startup permission. Tested on one Tang and API32; driving predictions and other firmware remain unverified.

No APK OTA from automotive.6. Keep your signing key; moving from the old signer requires reinstalling and clears local data.
