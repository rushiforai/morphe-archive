✨ Maps Automotive for BYD — Morphe preview 10 (TEST)

A 3D BYD Tang 2024 now drives in navigation instead of the arrow. Turn it off or pick a colour in the cluster settings → 3D car on map. With the 3D car off, Maps shows a flat car icon instead of the blue arrow.

The model is adapted from "2024 BYD Tang" by Ddiaz Design (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) and has our own rig and animations. The Google/Gemini patch is unchanged.

To update, repatch Maps from the same original with the same key.

The photo was taken on an Android 12 test emulator during navigation. The 3D car with this rig has not yet been tested in a car; the same model ran on a BYD Tang (DiLink 5.0) in earlier private test builds.
