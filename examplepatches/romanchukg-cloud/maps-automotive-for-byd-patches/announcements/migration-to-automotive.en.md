🗺 Google Maps for BYD: moving to Automotive

We are ending support for our phone edition and focusing on Automotive for Android head units.

Build updates yourself as soon as our patch supports a new Maps version—no need to wait for our ready-made APK.

1. Install Morphe Manager and add our TEST source below.
2. Select a clean original Maps Automotive 26.09.280001.E APK, not phone Maps or a patched APK. Patch and Save.
3. Install MicroG-RE 7.2.1 and your new APK.

Phone Maps can stay. Moving from our old Automotive to your own signing key requires reinstalling it, clearing its local data. Back up your key for updates. Keep old microG if other apps need it.

Morphe: https://github.com/MorpheApp/morphe-manager/releases
Add our TEST patch to Morphe: https://morphe.software/add-source?github=romanchukg-cloud/maps-automotive-for-byd-patches&name=Maps%20Automotive%20for%20BYD%20%28TEST%29
Full steps: https://github.com/romanchukg-cloud/maps-automotive-for-byd-patches/blob/main/MIGRATION.md
