✨ Maps Automotive for BYD — Morphe preview 7 (TEST)

Gemini over the map: start Live or Microphone with one tap or a wheel button, and press again to close it. Maps now repairs Google's voice service when it is stuck after the car starts.

Also new: a horizontal cluster pointer offset and assignable wheel buttons for zoom and Gemini.

Needs Maps Automotive 26.10.120001.E. Gemini also needs the pinned Google app, API 32+, MicroG-RE 7.2.2+, one signing key for both apps and Google allowed to self-start.

Tested on one BYD Tang with DiLink 5.0: test builds installed as updates with data kept, and compact Gemini opened. The auto-repair, second-press close, horizontal pointer and button assignment are not yet tested in the car.
