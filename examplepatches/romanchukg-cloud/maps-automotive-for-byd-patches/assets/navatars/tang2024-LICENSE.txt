The author's page states that the model is based on a BYD 3D model from downloadfree3d.com.

Changes: the interior removed, the body simplified to about 4000 triangles, the materials turned into
one 128x128 palette texture (red rear lamps brightened, body paint grey), and the result written as a
Navatars FBX with our own rig and animations. This adapted model is shared under the same licence,
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, for non-commercial use only. It is separate from the GPL-3.0 patch code.
