# GCamVBoost
Pixel Camera patches for Morphe: Video Boost

Adds:
- Video Boost toggle is now persistent
- Adds additional launcher shortcuts to open Video mode with VB on/off

Downsides:
- Magic Capture and Add Me features do not utilize EdgeTPU (limitation of cloning the app) so they may be slower and/or be less optimal. Perhaps best to keep the system camera enabled for these features.
