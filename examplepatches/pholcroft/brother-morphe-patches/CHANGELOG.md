# Changelog

## 0.2.2 (2026-11-18)

- Redesigned the saved printer cards, activity history and diagnostics screens. Printer cards now highlight the latest likely print preview and show the request count beside the time saved.
- Browse numbered requests, expand long responses and tap to copy the full value. More printer settings and responses have readable names, including MAC addresses and supported paper sizes.
- Record live printer responses with diagnostics, export a recording as CSV, or export individual bursts and activity history as JSON.

## 0.2.1 (2026-11-17)

- Faster print previews with saved printer responses and activity diagnostics.
