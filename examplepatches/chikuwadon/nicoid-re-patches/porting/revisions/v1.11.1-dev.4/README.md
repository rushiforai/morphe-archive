# nicoid Re v1.11.1-dev.4

Development-only build after v1.11.1-dev.3.

- The ambient halo in normal playback is composed as the background of the
  native player column rather than a window-level foreground overlay.
- The video information panel, control backgrounds, text, icons, and status
  bar retain their original stacking, including landscape tablet layouts.
- Popup's limited internal lighting and fullscreen's existing appearance
  remain unchanged.
- Ambient ON/OFF tap responsiveness from dev.3 is preserved.

Build, regression testing, and on-device inspection are required.
Do not publish a release or modify the stable v1.11.0 tag.
