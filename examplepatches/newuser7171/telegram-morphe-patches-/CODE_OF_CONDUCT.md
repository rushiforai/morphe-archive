# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Keep discussions respectful, technical, and constructive. Focus criticism on code, patches, documentation, and reproducible behavior—not on people.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient.
- Provide reproducible technical information.
- Give maintainers and contributors credit for their work.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials or private user data.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.
