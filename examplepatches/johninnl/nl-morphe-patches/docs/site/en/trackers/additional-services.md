# Other measurement services

These names describe different forms of measurement. Coverage is app-specific; use the app page to check the selected patches.

<a id="audience-measurement"></a>

## Audience measurement

Comscore and Gemius measure audience reach and activity. Named destinations include `scorecardresearch.com` and `gabe.hit.gemius.pl`. The exact service coverage differs between news apps.

<a id="editorial-analytics"></a>

## Reading and viewing analytics

Chartbeat, Parse.ly and Smartocto measure how content is used. Named destinations include `ping.chartbeat.net`, `pong.chartbeat.net`, `p1.parsely.com`, `ingestion.smartocto.com` and `ingestion.contentinsights.com`. Measurement and content recommendations can be separate functions.

<a id="media-telemetry"></a>

## Playback measurements

Blue Billywig, JWPlayer and publisher media services can measure playback in addition to delivering media. Named destinations include `stats.bluebillywig.com`, `psti.prd.dpge.be` and `tracker.prd.dpge.be/track`. Audio and video coverage can differ. A player may still exchange queue, device and playback-position information to keep a feature working.

<a id="publisher-measurement"></a>

## Publisher measurement

Publishers can run analytics under their own domains. FTM names Matomo at `insight.ftm.nl` and Eventor at `eventor.ftm.nl`. Omroep Brabant also has its own reading measurement. A publisher-owned address can serve measurement alongside useful app services.

<a id="publisher-identity"></a>

## Publisher identity

DMOI connects publisher identifiers with optional advertising, device or external-account identifiers. The named identity service is `dmoi.api.dpgmedia.net`. Reducing optional identity links does not remove the accounts needed for signing in.

<a id="piano"></a>

## Piano Analytics

Piano Analytics measures page views, interactions and media use. Records can include content, session or visitor information. The app page describes the optional measurement affected and the features retained.

<a id="experiments-and-surveys"></a>

## Experiments and advertising surveys

BrandMetrics measures advertising effectiveness and surveys; VWO supports experiments with different presentations. Named destinations include `phx.bmtrcs.com` and `dev.visualwebsiteoptimizer.com`. A blocked service does not mean a particular survey or experiment ran for every user.
