# Didomi

Didomi supplies privacy notices and consent preferences. Its services include configuration and choice management as well as optional service metrics. A consent patch may skip automatic screens while retaining manual preferences.



See your [app page](../apps/index.md) for the changes and any feature tradeoffs. A service's general capabilities do not establish that every app uses all of them.
