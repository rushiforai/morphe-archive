# RevenueCat

RevenueCat handles subscriptions, purchase receipts and access to paid features. It also supports optional customer attributes and analytics. Reducing optional measurement can leave genuine purchases and subscription checks available. The tracking patch does not create a subscription or purchase.

Named services include `api.revenuecat.com`, `api-paywalls.revenuecat.com` and `api-diagnostics.revenuecat.com`; their roles and the patch coverage differ.

See your [app page](../apps/index.md) for the changes and any feature tradeoffs. A service's general capabilities do not establish that every app uses all of them.
