# Contentsquare and Heap

Contentsquare and Heap measure how people use an app, including screen views and interactions. Depending on the setup, measurements can be associated with session, installation or account identifiers. Their presence does not mean every screen is recorded as a video.



See your [app page](../apps/index.md) for the changes and any feature tradeoffs. A service's general capabilities do not establish that every app uses all of them.
