# Bitmovin

Bitmovin provides video playback and separate viewing-quality analytics. Analytics can measure playback, buffering and errors. Blocking optional analytics can leave video playback available; advertisements and access to paid content are separate controls.



See your [app page](../apps/index.md) for the changes and any feature tradeoffs. A service's general capabilities do not establish that every app uses all of them.
