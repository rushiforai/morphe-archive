# OneTrust

OneTrust provides privacy notices and preference screens. It retrieves privacy settings and helps store choices. Hiding its automatic screen and reducing optional tracking are separate changes; manual privacy settings can remain available where the app supports them.

Buienradar retains consent-service destinations including `mobile-data.onetrust.io` and `geolocation.1trust.app`. A location-related hostname alone does not show which location data is shared.

See your [app page](../apps/index.md) for the changes and any feature tradeoffs. A service's general capabilities do not establish that every app uses all of them.
