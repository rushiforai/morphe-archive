## Patches

| Patch | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Disable tracking** | On | Stops the supported optional usage statistics, advertising identifiers and diagnostics. |
| **Hide Premium banner** | On | Hides automatic subscription offers and Premium promotions. |
| **Hide ads** | On | Hides automatic shop-product announcements while keeping editorial content. |
| **Read full articles** | On | Displays complete article text when the publisher supplies it. Does not create a subscription. |
| **Skip consent screens** | On | Skips the cookie notice and account offer after edition selection. Manual login remains available. |

## Advertising and tracking services

The ad and tracking controls cover the supported optional activity from: Matomo (insight.ftm.nl), Eventor (eventor.ftm.nl), Sentry / GlitchTip (glitchtip.ftm.nl), ActiveCampaign; notification measurement.

Blocking measurement does not block every function from the same provider. Essential app services can still connect.

## Before using

Only article text supplied by FTM can be displayed. Manual membership and login links remain available.
