## Patches

| Patch | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Guest session playback host** | On | Lets guests play supported episodes without signing in. Use with Unlock Premium playback. |
| **Unlock Premium playback** | On | Replaces Premium buttons with play buttons, enables playing supported episodes and hides trial offers. |

## Before using

Use both patches together. They do not create a subscription. The service can still refuse playback; downloads, audiobooks and availability across regions or accounts are not guaranteed. No tracking-blocking patch is included.
