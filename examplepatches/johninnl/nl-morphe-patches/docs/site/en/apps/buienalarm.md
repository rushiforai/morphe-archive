## Patches

| Patch | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Disable tracking** | On | Stops the supported optional usage statistics, advertising identifiers and diagnostics. |
| **Fix Google services** | On | Keeps Google Maps and the supported Google services working in the patched app. |
| **Hide Premium banner** | On | Hides the Premium promotion card in Settings. |
| **Hide ads** | On | Removes banners, full-screen ads and the empty space reserved for banners. |
| **Skip consent screens** | On | Skips automatic advertising-consent screens; manual privacy choices remain available. |

## Advertising and tracking services

The ad and tracking controls cover the supported optional activity from: Firebase, Meta, RevenueCat, Didomi; publisher advertising targeting.

Blocking measurement does not block every function from the same provider. Essential app services can still connect.

## Before using

Weather, alerts and purchases still use online services. These patches do not grant Premium.
