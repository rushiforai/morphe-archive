## Patches

| Patch | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Disable tracking** | On | Stops the supported optional usage statistics, advertising identifiers and diagnostics. |
| **Fix PairIP startup** | On | Lets the patched app start and use its supported features after installation. Keep this enabled. |
| **Hide ads** | On | Removes the supported ads before or around videos. Ads already included in the programme or stream may remain. |

## Advertising and tracking services

The ad and tracking controls cover the supported optional activity from: NPO Tag / Topspin, NMO DAM, STER targeting, Sentry, Bitmovin, Google / Firebase; Cast diagnostics.

Blocking measurement does not block every function from the same provider. Essential app services can still connect.

## Before using

Use the correct phone or Android TV build. Accounts and content access still follow the service’s rules. These patches do not grant a subscription; broadcast, stream-inserted or Cast-receiver ads may remain.
