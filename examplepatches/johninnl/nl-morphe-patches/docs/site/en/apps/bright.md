## Patches

| Patch | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Disable tracking** | On | Stops the supported optional usage statistics, advertising identifiers and diagnostics. |
| **Hide ads** | On | Removes banners, their empty space, full-screen ads before articles and sponsored editorial (#SPON) entries in the feed. Website and video ads are outside this control. |
| **Read full articles** | On | Opens supported Bright articles in a clean reader with full text, cover and inline images, captions and supported video playback without a separate video-consent screen. Unsupported content keeps the original view. |
| **Skip consent screens** | On | Skips the advertising-consent screens and the cookie popup on Bright article pages. The age check remains. |

## Advertising and tracking services

The ad and tracking controls cover the supported optional activity from: Google Ad Manager banners and interstitials; Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sessions and Performance; advertising identifiers.

Blocking measurement does not block every function from the same provider. Essential app services can still connect.

## Before using

Use the complete ARM64 APKS. Unsupported videos, live streams, protected media and interactive content may keep the original view. Website/video advertising and website tracking are not covered. No subscription is created.
