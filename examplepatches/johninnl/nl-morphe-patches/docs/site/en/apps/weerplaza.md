## Patches

| Patch | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Disable tracking** | On | Stops the supported optional usage statistics, advertising identifiers and diagnostics. |
| **Fix Google Maps** | On | Keeps Google Maps working in the patched app. |
| **Hide Premium banner** | On | Hides automatic subscription offers and Premium promotions. |
| **Hide ads** | On | Removes the supported advertising placements while keeping ordinary content available. |
| **Skip consent screens** | On | Skips automatic advertising-consent screens; manual privacy choices remain available. |

## Advertising and tracking services

The ad and tracking controls cover the supported optional activity from: Google advertising, PubMatic OpenWrap, Firebase, Blue Billywig, ScorecardResearch, Sentry, PushAlert, Didomi, RevenueCat, Google tags; analytics.infraloods.nl.

Blocking measurement does not block every function from the same provider. Essential app services can still connect.

## Before using

Paid hourly data still requires the publisher to provide access. Weather, maps, purchases and push services remain available.
