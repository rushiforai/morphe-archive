## Patches

| Patch | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Disable tracking** | On | Stops the supported optional usage statistics, advertising identifiers and diagnostics. |
| **Hide ads** | On | Removes the supported advertising placements while keeping ordinary content available. |
| **Skip consent screens** | On | Removes automatic cookie and notification prompts and the cookie-settings menu entry. Notifications can be enabled manually. |

## Advertising and tracking services

The ad and tracking controls cover the supported optional activity from: Outbrain, Start.io, Blue Billywig, Firebase; publisher reading and viewing statistics.

Blocking measurement does not block every function from the same provider. Essential app services can still connect.
