## Patches

| Patch | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Disable tracking** | On | Stops the supported optional usage statistics, advertising identifiers and diagnostics. |
| **Hide Premium banner** | On | Hides automatic subscription offers and Premium promotions. |
| **Hide ads** | On | Removes banners, sponsored placements and video ads. |
| **Skip consent screens** | On | Skips automatic advertising-consent screens; manual privacy choices remain available. |
| **Unlock Plus** | On | Enables local Plus options, including dashboard tiles, their order and rain-alert settings. |

## Advertising and tracking services

The ad and tracking controls cover the supported optional activity from: Google Mobile Ads, Meta / Audience Network, Firebase, Blue Billywig, Google Tag Manager, Didomi, RevenueCat.

Blocking measurement does not block every function from the same provider. Essential app services can still connect.

## Before using

Unlock Plus does not create a purchase or guarantee server-delivered rain alerts. Tapping some locked features can still show a Plus explanation.
