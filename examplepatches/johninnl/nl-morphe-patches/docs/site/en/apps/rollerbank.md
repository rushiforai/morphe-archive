## Patches

| Patch | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Enable En Route alerts** | On | Enables En Route access and server-loaded static-camera warnings, requests notification permission, and keeps sound and visual warnings independent of speech startup. |
| **Fix Google services** | On | Keeps Google Maps and Firebase registration compatible with the patched app. Included by the other controls. |
| **Hide ads** | On | Removes banner and native advertising and their list placements. |
| **Disable tracking** | On | Includes ad removal. Stops supported optional usage events, diagnostics, identifiers and session measurement. |
| **Skip consent screens** | On | Removes the advertising consent button and requirement from Home setup. Includes ad and tracking controls. |
| **Hide Premium banner** | On | Skips the Premium introduction, removes settings Upgrades and removes the Premium/Remove ads overflow menu from every main page. |

## Advertising and tracking services

The controls cover supported optional activity from Google Mobile Ads, Google consent handling, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Firebase Sessions. Functional authentication, report requests, maps and push registration can still connect.

## Before using

Location and notification permissions remain optional during introduction. Nearby and area filters still require their normal location or area settings. Removing advertising consent does not grant consent on your behalf.

Enable En Route alerts uses the app's real nearby reports and static-camera data. Static-camera warnings are enabled on first use; later changes in En Route settings are kept. Android location and notification permissions are still required. Camera coverage and alert delivery depend on the server, location accuracy and system settings. Purchase status remains unchanged.
