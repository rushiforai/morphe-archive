# How tracking works

An app can record actions such as opening a screen, reading an article or starting a video. Such a record is often called an **event**. A service may combine events with time, device information and an [identifier](identifiers.md) to recognise repeated activity.

Services have different purposes. Advertising networks deliver advertisements, audience tools count reach, analytics tools measure use, and crash-reporting services collect information about failures. Content, account and notification services also exchange data to provide features.

Patches reduce the optional collection described on each [app page](../apps/index.md). Removing ads and reducing tracking are separate choices. Keeping an online feature can also mean keeping the data exchange it needs.

The [service profiles](../trackers/index.md) explain the named providers. The [scope and limitations](../reference/methodology.md) explain what the patch names promise.
