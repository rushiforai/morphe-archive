# Consent, ads and tracking

**Hide ads**, **Disable tracking** and **Skip consent screens** have different purposes. Hiding an advertisement does not necessarily stop other measurements in the app. Skipping a privacy screen does not by itself describe which data is shared.

Where supported, the consent patch skips automatic privacy prompts together with its required ad and tracking controls. Some apps also skip introductory login or notification prompts. Existing choices and access to manual settings vary by app; check the [app page](../apps/index.md).

[OneTrust](../trackers/onetrust.md) and [Didomi](../trackers/didomi.md) provide privacy-choice screens. These services differ from advertising networks and analytics providers.

Use [choosing patches](../guides/choose-patches.md) to compare the available controls. Patching does not withdraw an existing agreement with a service or delete its historical records.
