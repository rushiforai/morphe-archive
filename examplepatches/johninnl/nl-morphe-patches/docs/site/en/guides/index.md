# Use the patches

1. Find your app and [download the correct original file](downloads.md).
2. Follow the [installation guide](install.md).
3. [Choose your patches](choose-patches.md) and read their effects on the app page.

For help, see [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

Additional setup: [Android Auto](android-auto.md), [Shizuku on a phone](shizuku.md) and [Shizuku on Android TV](shizuku-tv.md).
