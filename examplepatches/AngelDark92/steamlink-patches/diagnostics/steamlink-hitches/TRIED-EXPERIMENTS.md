## Current direction

**All three standalone hitch experiments are retired (2026-09-19).** Decoder input buffering (staging/observe), the FEC duplicate reservation guard, and the UDP receive buffer increase were tested by the user on the newer Steam builds and **did not solve the hitching problem**; the UDP trial made freezes worse and longer. Their patch code, tests, standalone audit runners and bundled helper payloads were removed from `patches/src/main` on 2026-09-19, and the committed catalogs no longer list them. Do not re-add, re-import, or re-derive any of them — including telemetry-only or check-only variants. The [FEC experiment record](EXPERIMENT-2026-09-15-fec-duplicate-reservation.md) and [proposal](RECOVERY-PATCH-PROPOSAL-2026-09-15.md) remain as evidence; the host 300 ms throttle/retransmit-history amplification, gated PC reset-priority candidate, and legacy-transport selector they describe were never shipped and remain unproven.

Stock UDP behavior is restored by any original-APK rebuild from current source. Investigate transport/FEC frame lifecycle and reset generations: current freezes include repeated old frame work, exhausted decoder input indices, flushes and watchdog recovery despite 0 observed receive-buffer drops. Packet age and the first upstream cause remain unresolved; do not equate the observed UDP-trial regression with proven UDP-induced backlog.

Rationale and version/host boundaries: [Android XR and PC comparison](XR-HOST-COMPARISON-2026-09-15.md). Direct socket-drop and decoder-gap evidence: [Observe results](OBSERVE-RESULTS-2026-09-15.md).
