# Restored: Steam Link 2.0.20/5001812, 2.0.21/5001968 and 2.0.22/5002244 (2026-10-22)

The three bases retired on 2026-10-21 are supported again. This record supplements the
historical [README](README.md) and [retirement note](RETIRED-2026-10-21.md); history is not
rewritten.

> **2026-11-01:** the GXR spacewarp layer was retired and deleted. The spacewarp items
> below are historical. See the
> [retirement record](../steamlink-spacewarp-retirement/README.md).

## What was restored

- Exact compatibility entries for 5001812, 5001968 and 5002244, plus the shared legacy
  recommendation list. All four pre-2363 builds now recommend the same 21 patches.
  OpenXR CPU and GPU BOOST is experimental, default off, and outside every recommended set.
- Native tables from the pre-retirement commit `6d19288^`:
  - Visual Delay HMD hook/velocity layouts.
  - Blue-noise shader sites and background draw-return cases.
  - OLED video layouts and swapchain format offsets.
  - Native permission-name and force-gate edits.
  - Permission-prompt prologue offsets.
  - Microphone preset offsets.
- 1812/1968 complete-function identities in `AddedLegacyNativeGuards.kt`.
- Codecs Support profiles for 1812/1968/2244 (new, required for recommendation parity).
- Network Optimizations, Controller pose time, GXR spacewarp and telemetry profiles for the
  restored builds.
- Foveal canvas scene layouts for 1812/1968/2244.

## Identity change

`Device identity (2.0.23/5002363)` and its native upsert code were removed. Stock 2.0.23
identity is authoritative. `Device identity` remains a legacy-only recommended patch. The
`galaxyXrVendorDriverPatch` keeps its own hmd/controller edits and no longer depends on a
native identity patch.

## Validation

- `:patches:test --rerun-tasks`: 211 tests, 19 skipped, 0 failures. The skipped cases are retained-input audits whose exact decoded trees or Morphe audit inputs are absent on this host.
- `:patches:buildAndroid :patches:generatePatchesList -PreleaseChannel=experimental`: passed. Catalogs now contain 47 patches (40 stable, 47 experimental/all).
- `auditDecodedSteamLinkPatches` reaches the standalone high-resolution case and fails its DEX-count assertion (`5001712: standalone high resolution added or removed DEX files`). That is a pre-existing Morphe resource-only output limitation, not a compatibility edit: adding any bytecode patch to the selection (the recommended set does) restores the DEX payload. It is recorded as an open audit gap, not as a pass.
- Codecs, telemetry, pose-time and spacewarp payloads were rebuilt with the pinned NDK and their SHA-256 pins updated together.
- 2363 Network Optimizations output hashes remain `4d5ff693…` (scene) and `17c1a4d9…` (main).
- Headset validation is pending for 5001812, 5001968 and 5002244, and for the newly ported experimental layers on every base.
